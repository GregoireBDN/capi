# Budgets and envelopes

Feature details moved out of [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md) (#422). The domain rules, workflow conventions and architecture there apply here too.

- **Shared expenses** (#359): budget lines and envelopes read the owner's part (`transaction_lines`); `BudgetLineDto.grossSpentCents` is what was paid before others' shares, for the detail (« 400 € sur 800 € payés »).
- **Planned income** (#315): a budget line on an **income** category is the month's planned income (salary, benefits, a family transfer…), in the same `budgets` table and with the same rules as expense targets (effective-dated from a month on, carried over to later months without a line of their own, a note, `DELETE` ends it); a transfer category is refused (400) and an income line is never `tracked` (400 when asked). `GET /api/budgets` keeps the expense targets in `items` / `totals` and adds `income` (each line planned vs received from `transaction_lines` with kind `income`, so splits count and transfers or refunds filed in expenses never do; `unplannedCents` outside the plan; `receivedCents` null for an empty month) and `plan` (`toSaveCents` « À épargner » = planned income − budgeted expenses, null without planned income). A partial month is read like for like (`budgets/income.ts`) only where the day is known, from a confirmed recurring credit of the category (monthly, quarterly, yearly): `expectedByNowCents`, `expectedOn`, `status` (`pending`, `received`, `short`, `missing`, `so_far` « reçu à ce jour »). `GET /api/budgets/income-suggestions?month=` prepares a month's income (the plan carried over, else the confirmed recurring credits at their monthly equivalent, rounded to the euro); `history` adds `income` and `plannedIncomeCents` / `receivedIncomeCents` / `plannedSavingsCents` per month. The budget page (#434, one question per page: « Suis-je dans mes objectifs ? ») opens on its answer, « Reste à dépenser » (« Dépassement » once over) as its big figure (`BudgetSummary`, an `AnswerFigure`: the total's meter, then « n dépassés » red and « n à surveiller » amber, nothing else: what lies outside the budget is « Hors budget »'s card, what was spent per category Mouvements'), then « Dépenses prévues » (the envelope lines and the category lines), then « Revenus prévus » (`IncomePlanCard`: each line, what came in, the totals, « À épargner »; « Préparer les revenus », `IncomeSuggestionsSheet`), « Hors budget » and the history. The month close lists the income lines that did not come in (`income.shortfalls`) and freezes `report.plan` (planned vs received income, planned vs actual savings; null in reports frozen before) with the income categories' planned amount as their `targetCents`.
- A budget line can be **tracked** (`budgets.tracked`, "suivre pendant le mois", carried over like the note): the dashboard's « Budgets suivis » (#430, « Enveloppes » before) (`GET /api/budgets/envelopes?month=`, default the current Europe/Paris month, `current` says which; the dashboard reads the current month only since #405) shows spent / target, what's left and, for the current month, the even-pace mark (target × day / days in the month); a past month's pace is the whole target and nothing is ahead of it, a future month's is 0. With `?all=true` it gives every expense line of the month, tracked or not (`tracked` says which), for « Budgets suivis » on categories chosen in the dashboard's settings (#495, [`months-and-dashboard.md`](months-and-dashboard.md)).
- **Envelopes pay** (#429, [ADR 0008](../adr/0008-envelopes.md), [`envelopes.md`](envelopes.md)): an expense attached to an envelope keeps its category and counts once in `spentCents`; what the envelope paid (`envelopes/paid.ts`, attributed to the expense's lines in order, each at most the owner's part) is the line's `envelopePaidCents` with the envelopes that paid (`envelopes`), and the totals' `envelopePaidCents`. It adds to the month's budget of the category: `remainingCents` = target + paid by envelopes − spent, and `percentUsed` / `status` read what the budget paid itself (spent − paid by envelopes). The tracked lines (`GET /api/budgets/envelopes`, the dashboard's « Budgets suivis ») read it the same way (remaining, pace, status) and carry `envelopePaidCents`. The month close's frozen report is unchanged. On the page (#430): a category line shows « + 95 € par Lisbonne » beside its own share and each envelope that paid is a line of its own ([`envelopes.md`](envelopes.md#screens-430)).
