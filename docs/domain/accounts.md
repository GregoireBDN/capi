# Accounts

Feature details moved out of [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md) (#422). The domain rules, workflow conventions and architecture there apply here too.

## Meal vouchers

- **Meal vouchers** (#298, option 1 of the issue): the employer's card is a `meal_voucher` account whose transactions are all entered by hand (`ManualTransactionSheet`, MCP `add_transaction`): the monthly credit is `income` in the seeded « Titres-restaurant » category (`meal_vouchers`, detected as a recurring credit), each payment an `expense` in its real category (restaurants, groceries), never a transfer, so spending, budgets, the month review and the savings rate count both sides. Its balance is the sum of its transactions, or a balance snapshot plus what is booked after it (the editor's optional « Solde actuel » is stored as the balance at the end of yesterday; « Corriger le solde » on its page). It is in net worth as cash (money the owner holds, spendable on food only), never in the forecast (`GET /api/forecast` refuses it) nor in `coverage/` (manual only). Its page (`MealVoucherAccount.tsx`) has « Ajouter un paiement » / « Ajouter un crédit » in its « ⋮ » (#386), then the balance and the latest operations; the entry sheet offers it as « Payé avec » for restaurants and groceries and puts the credit on it. Its payments are capped at **25 € a day** (`MEAL_VOUCHER_DAILY_CAP_CENTS`, the legal cap): `POST|PATCH /transactions/manual` answers 400 (`capped at 25 €`) when the card's expenses out of one day, the edited one counted once, would go over it (credits and refunds never are); the sheet shows the day as a gauge (`VoucherDayGauge`: the 25 € bar with the day's card payments from `voucherDayQuery`, the edited one left out, then this one, red when over; « Reste 5 € » or « 13 € max ») on the amount and details steps, and greys out « Ajouter » over it: no error text, no toast. The provider's export and expiry dates are not handled.

## Archive or delete

- **Archive or delete** (#297): archiving (`PATCH /api/accounts/:id` `archived`) is the default for an account that was used, keeping its history. `DELETE /api/accounts/:id` (session only `@TokenAccess('none')`, fresh session `FreshAuthGuard`; no MCP tool) removes, in one DB transaction, its transactions with their splits, imports, exchange imports (their movements cascade), recurring items, snapshots and savings goal links, then the account, whose other crypto data (holdings, wallet keys and tokens, scans) and account-scoped rules cascade (`imports` and `crypto_imports` restrict the account's deletion, #367). Budgets and categories are untouched; closed months keep their frozen report (their `changes` show the difference). `GET /api/accounts/:id/deletion-preview` counts all of it plus the closed months holding its data. The web: « Archiver ou supprimer » at the end of `AccountEditor` (archive first), then `DeleteAccountScreen` in the same sheet: the counts, « Définitif », the account's name to type (case and spacing ignored), `ReauthSheet` on the fresh-session 403, a toast and back to « Comptes ». No undo.

## A cash account's page

- A **cash account's page** (#293, `components/accounts/CashAccount.tsx`, one column at every width, owner's choice) answers « where does it stand, is it up to date? »: the header names the institution only, its page actions are « Importer un relevé » (`/import?account=`), « Ajouter une opération » (`ManualTransactionSheet` `initialAccountId`) and « Modifier »; then the balance with « À jour au … · importé il y a … » (a warning when the bank data stops before the previous month end) and the month's money in / out / change (`GET /api/accounts/:id/month-flows`: every movement, transfers included and apart, bank values), the daily balance over 12 months with the statement balances as dots (`GET /api/accounts/:id/balance-history`, `accounts/balance-history.ts`: the anchors of `loadBalances` as of each day, so the last point is the balance; also the window's running-balance gaps, `dataUntil` and the last import day), the latest transactions (`TransactionRow` without selection), the latest imports and gaps, and the confirmed recurring debits paid from it. Investment and crypto pages share the header, their actions in `PageHeader` too.
- **The typed values are a screen of their own** (#420, the owner: the list took room while rarely read): « Historique des valeurs » in the page's actions (`ValueHistorySheet`, full screen on phones, a centred modal elsewhere) lists newest first an investment's values (each with the invested amount that counts), a bank account's statement balances or a meal-voucher card's corrections; a tap opens `SnapshotEditor` over it (correct, delete), and it offers « Ajouter une valeur » / « Ajouter un solde de relevé » and « Coller un historique ». No values list on the account pages. Crypto accounts have none (their values are computed, #366).

## Account lists

- **Account lists** (#267): Épargne and Comptes draw their accounts with `AccountList` / `AccountListRow` (`components/accounts/AccountList.tsx`, on `ItemList` from `@finances/ui`): no rules, one target per row (account page, or a cash account's transactions on the dashboard) with its chevron last, a 32px type badge (`ACCOUNT_TYPE_ICONS`), left the name and « institution · au 31 oct. » (the type only when neither the group nor the name says it), both cut with … when they don't fit, right the value and the gain over contributions or « à mettre à jour », never cut (#262, owner's « Pastille + montant »); on a row under 464px (every phone) `AccountListRow` takes two lines (`ItemListRow` `compactWhenNarrow` + `compactDetail`, #344, owner's « D. Nom puis chiffres », after « C. Empilé » was found too tall): the name alone, then the value and the rate in the gain's colour, or the stale value's date in the warning colour (« ● 30 sept. »); the badge stays centred, the gaps tighten to 8px so a 20-character name fits at 375px, and « institution · date » and the gain in euros are left to wider rows and the account page. No edit button in a list: a value is updated on the account page (« Mettre à jour la valeur », primary for hand-valued accounts), the month close and `StaleValuesReminder`; the dashboard's « À faire » opens Comptes for the values to update. The add buttons are stacked at full width, one primary per page (`primaryAction`).
