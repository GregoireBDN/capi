# Crypto

Feature details moved out of [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md) (#422). The domain rules, workflow conventions and architecture there apply here too.

## Holdings and prices

- Prices come from a key-less public API (CoinGecko `simple/price`, behind the `PriceProvider` interface), **server-side only**, all assets batched in one request, cached in `crypto_prices` for `PRICE_CACHE_TTL_SECONDS` (default 600) and kept as last known price when the API fails (flagged stale, never a 500). `PRICE_API_ENABLED=false` disables it: only manual prices (`PUT /api/crypto/prices/:assetId`) are used. The same request carries each price's change over 24 hours (#295, `include_24hr_change`, `PriceProvider.fetchEurQuotes`): `crypto_prices.change_24h_percent` (`numeric(12, 4)`, signed, read from the JSON text with `signedDecimalFromJsonNumber`, an unreadable one left null and never fatal), null for a manual price; shown per asset (`priceChange24hPercent` on valued holdings) and summed into what the portfolio moved.

## Cost basis and observed movements

- **The cost follows the coins** (#373, `crypto/cost-basis.ts` `replayCosts`, loaded by `CryptoCostBasisService` in `crypto/cost-basis.service.ts`): every crypto account keeps, **per asset**, a pool of quantity and cost (average cost, « PMP »), moved by **one replay of every movement of every crypto account** (archived ones too) in date order, exact (`bigint` quantities at 18 decimals, integer cents, half to even): a buy adds its euro cost (fees included), income comes at 0; a sale into euros removes its share of the cost and its proceeds minus that share is a **realised gain** (computed on read, never stored: it changes when an earlier cost is answered; `RealisedGainsDto` year / total, « Plus-value réalisée »); a conversion (or a sale into another coin) carries the cost to the coin it brings in; a **transfer between two of the owner's accounts** (a link of `crypto_transfer_links`: a `withdrawal` or `observed_out` to a `deposit` or `observed_in`) takes its share of the cost out of the source at the earlier of its two sides and puts it **whole** into the destination at its arrival, a network fee paid in coins lowering the quantity, never the cost; coins whose cost the app can't know (a deposit or an arrival nobody answered, an opening position beyond what was linked before it) are counted apart (« 0,1 BTC sans coût connu »), leave in proportion so the known part keeps its average, and are left out of the gain, the others still counted; **euros waiting on an exchange** (its EUR lines, or for Coinbase, which exports none, a buy's total spent from the balance as far as it goes and a sale's proceeds credited) count in its value and its invested amount at face value. A crypto account's invested amount at a moment (`cryptoInvestedOn` / `cryptoInvestedNow`, `InvestedStep`) is the cost of its coins of known cost plus its euros, so **the total over the crypto accounts moves only with buys, sales and costs given, never with transfers**. A wallet's **opening** (its first read, `observed_since` null) reconciles: what was linked to the account alone before its first read (#296 `counterpart_account_id`, e.g. `linkNewCryptoTransfers`) is there at its cost, the rest is of unknown cost unless the opening was answered, and anything below what was sent is fees (within `FEE_TOLERANCE_PERCENT`, 5 %) or else left at average cost; after the first read only a link carries cost. `crypto_holdings.invested_cents` stays a per-line detail of lines typed by hand; a line read from an export or a chain shows its share of its asset's cost (`allocateCosts`), and `AccountHoldingsDto.costs` the cost of each asset (« Prix de revient »: `costCents`, `unknownQuantity`, its value and gain), `totals.unknown` / `fiatCents`, `realised`. `GET /api/investments/movements` gives a withdrawal the cost the replay moved.
- **Observed movements and matching** (#373): a wallet's **complete** read (every chain of every key this server reads, none failed: a failed or partial read, a Bitcoin scan cut short, records nothing) is compared with the sum of its observed movements per asset (`CryptoCostBasisService.observe`, in the sync's transaction): each asset that rose or fell gets one `crypto_movements` row (`kind` `observed_in` / `observed_out`, no import, `asset_id`, exact signed quantity, `occurred_at` the read, `observed_since` the account's previous complete read, `accounts.crypto_observed_at`), nothing when unchanged, never an address, logs counting rows only; a manual account's line edits do the same (`operation` `manual_edit`), an arrival answered `bought` at the rise of its asset's typed invested amounts when every line has one. After each such read and each exchange import commit, **matching** (`crypto/transfer-matching.ts`) links a departure and an arrival of the same asset in two accounts when the arrival is at most what left and at least what a fee leaves (5 %), arrived after it left and at most `TRANSFER_MATCH_DAYS` (3, as bank transfers #296: exchanges process withdrawals within hours; the window counts from the previous read of the wallet, so an arrival seen weeks later still matches) after it, and the account either side names agrees; only pairs that match one way only: a tie links nothing. A link names each side's account on the other. Unlinking by hand (`DELETE /api/crypto-movements/:id/link`) or any answer sets `auto_match = false`, so a link removed by hand stays removed. **« À préciser »** (`crypto/pending/`, `GET /api/accounts/:id/crypto-pending`): per account, oldest first, the arrivals (« D'où viennent ces 0,1 BTC ? »), departures (« Où sont partis ces 0,1 BTC ? ») and openings' part of unknown cost not linked nor answered (an exchange's withdrawal or deposit naming another account is not asked), each with the other accounts' movements that could be its other side (`canPick`; an opening: withdrawals before it), and the answers given. `POST /api/crypto-movements/:id/answer` (`transfer` with `counterpartMovementId`: a manual link, or for an opening a withdrawal naming the account; `bought` with `costCents`; `gift`, cost 0; `disposed`, departures only), `DELETE …/answer` asks it again; all session only (answering stays in the app). Database rules (migration `0044`): `crypto_movements_observed_shape` (observed ⇔ no import ⇔ `asset_id`; `observed_since` on observed rows only), `crypto_movements_observed_sign`, `crypto_movements_resolution_kind`, `crypto_movements_given_cost` (`cost_cents` holds the cost the owner gave, 0 for a gift, nothing else; migration `0044` nulled the computed withdrawal costs), `crypto_transfer_links` one link per movement each side, trigger `crypto_transfer_links_sides` (a departure to an arrival of another account, neither answered, never an opening), trigger `crypto_movements_linked_unanswered`, `accounts_crypto_observed_only_wallet_or_manual`.
- **The cost on the crypto pages** (#373, web): every crypto account page (wallet, exchange, manual) shows **« À préciser »** right under its summary (`components/crypto/PendingCard.tsx`, hidden when there is nothing to say): one settings row per movement (« D’où viennent ces 0,1 BTC ? », « Où sont partis ces 0,05 BTC ? », « Prix de revient de vos 0,2 BTC arrivés avant le 1er avr. ? », with when it happened, `pending-text.ts`), a tap opening its answers in a stepped sheet (bottom sheet on phones), one decision per screen: an arrival « Depuis une plateforme » (only when a candidate exists, then the withdrawal to pick), « Achetés ailleurs » (then the cost, `AmountField`), « Reçus gratuitement »; a departure « Vers une plateforme » or « Vendus ou donnés »; every answer's toast offers « Annuler » and the answers given stay folded under « Réponses données (n) », each with « Annuler ». **« Prix de revient »** per asset: unfolded under each asset of a wallet's « Avoirs » and listed at the end of an exchange's or manual account's holdings card (`CostList` in `HoldingsPanel`, with the euros waiting on the platform), `costLines`: « Prix de revient 15 000,00 € · résultat +2 000,00 € », « dont 0,1 BTC sans coût connu ». **« Plus-value réalisée »** (this year, in all) closes the summary of an account that sold, and the Crypto page's « Valeur de vos cryptos » card reads like an account (the owner's « A. Comme un compte », 20 Nov, chosen over three tiles or one sentence): the value and its change over 24 hours, then « Investi » on the coins alone with its bar (`InvestedBar`), « Résultat » (euros and rate) and « Réalisé » (this year's realised gain, « en 2026 ») as two tiles (`Tile`, both from `InvestmentSummary`), the coins of unknown cost in one line (« Hors 0,1 BTC sans coût connu : à préciser sur la page du compte. »), and the euros waiting on the exchanges as a grey « Euros » part of the split bar, never a line of their own nor in « Investi ». A crypto account has no « Renseigner / Modifier le montant investi » (`InvestedSheet` is for PEA-like accounts only).

## Stored first, live second

- **Stored first, live second** (#381, `components/crypto/live-refresh.ts`): the crypto reads (`GET /api/crypto/portfolio`, `GET /api/crypto/summary`, `GET /api/accounts/:id/holdings`) answer **from the database only**: stored holdings, last known prices (`priceStale` after the TTL), last sync times, invested amounts; never a price request or a chain read (`crypto.service.spec.ts` builds them with a price provider and chain clients that fail if called). They say `refreshDue` when a live step would do something (a price older than the TTL not asked within it, `CryptoPricesService.refreshDue`; a wallet due a sync, `WalletSyncService.syncDueFor`; both from memory, no request). The live step is **a separate request the page fires after showing the stored values** (`useLiveRefresh`: once `refreshDue`, at most every 30 s per page): `POST /api/crypto/portfolio/refresh?mode=auto` (Crypto) or `POST /api/accounts/:id/holdings/valuate?mode=auto` (wallet, exchange and manual pages), which only syncs the wallets due and asks the prices older than the TTL, then answers the same DTO, written into the cache; `mode=force` (the default) stays « Actualiser ». It is silent (no toast); a failed request marks the cached data `refreshFailed`, so the stored values stay with the usual stale notes, never an error page. No route-loader prefetch (the session cookies don't reach the generated client during SSR): the React Query cache shows a page again at once, read again behind it. Loaders only where something loads: on a first load (nothing cached) one skeleton per card, shaped like it with its title (`components/crypto/skeletons.tsx`); once data is shown, the live step only replaces a status line in place (« Actualisation des cours… », « Lecture des chaînes… », « Wallet · lecture en cours… », the price age of « Avoirs »), and the stale notes (« Cours non actualisés », « Non à jour », « non à jour ») wait for it (`pricesSettling`, `staleShown`), so a visit after the TTL shows no flash of warnings; no card goes back to a skeleton.

## One value per day

- **One value per day, written on schedule** (#366, `crypto/daily-close/`): page loads, « Actualiser » and syncs only compute **today's live value** (holdings × the last known prices, `AccountHoldingsDto.snapshot`, never stored; `GET /api/accounts/:id/performance` gives it as `live`, the last point of the history, and counts it in the figures: value, change since yesterday's close, gain). The **daily close** (`CryptoDailyCloseService`, a plain `setTimeout` started by `main.ts` only, never by specs or scripts; `CRYPTO_DAILY_CLOSE_ENABLED=false` turns it off) writes one `computed` `balance_snapshot` per open crypto account and day, the holdings at the end of the day × that day's closing price, upserted on (account, day), never over a typed value: `CLOSE_MINUTES_AFTER_MIDNIGHT` (5) after midnight Europe/Paris for the days before, and on boot (10 s after) to catch up the days missed, up to `CLOSE_CATCH_UP_DAYS` (31) back; runs are serialized and idempotent (one API instance). Closing prices are kept in **`crypto_price_history`** (asset id, day, exact EUR price, `provider` / `manual`; `CryptoPriceHistoryService`): from the provider's daily history (`PriceProvider.fetchEurHistory`, CoinGecko `coins/:id/market_chart?interval=daily`, one request per asset, only its id sent, paced by `historyIntervalMs` (2 s), at most `PRICE_HISTORY_MAX_DAYS` = 365 days on the key-less API; a day's close is the point nearest to its end in Paris within 3 h: the 00:00 UTC point, or the current price right after midnight), else the manual price for the days from the one it was set (price API off, or an asset it does not know: 404). A missing price leaves the day without a point (never a 500); a failed request makes the close run again an hour later. It writes the value only: the invested amount is computed on read (#370). **Exchanges**: their history is rebuilt from their movements (`exchangeLinesByDay`: `computeHoldings` at the end of each day, plus the euros waiting from the replay, #373) over the 365 days the prices go back, on boot and in the background after an import is committed or undone (`requestRebuild`); older values stay. **Wallets and manual accounts**: their quantities at each day's end are what their observed movements (#373) had added up to by then; one without any yet (lines typed directly) counts its lines as they are, a line typed by hand from the day it was created; an edit is not backdated. A **first** value of 0 € (lines at 0, nothing synced yet) is never stored nor shown: the account stays "not valued yet" (#207). The net worth and the account lists read the stored values, so a crypto account shows yesterday's close there; the crypto pages show the live value. Logs carry counts only.

## Sources, wallet sync and the wallet page

- A crypto account has a **source** set at creation (`accounts.crypto_source`): `exchange` (with `crypto_provider` binance / coinbase / other; holdings from its imported exports), `wallet` (public keys in `crypto_wallet_keys`: Bitcoin xpub / ypub / zpub, a single Bitcoin address `1…` / `3…` / `bc1q…` / `bc1p…`, or EVM `0x` address, with the chains to read; holdings from the chain) or `manual` (typed by hand; accounts created before sources). A `manual` account holding nothing can become an exchange or a wallet once (`PUT /api/accounts/:id/crypto-source`, session only, #207; « Relier à un wallet ou une plateforme » in its « Source des avoirs » screen of « Plus d’informations », #447, `ChangeCryptoSourceSheet`); its 0 € values are dropped when it never had another one. `GET /api/crypto/settings` says whether prices and wallet sync are on, so the wallet forms warn when sync is off. Holdings carry an `origin` (`import`, `sync`, `manual`): imported and synced lines are read-only and imports / syncs only replace their own lines, never manual ones. A `wallet` account takes no line typed by hand (#269): `POST|PUT /api/accounts/:id/holdings[/:holdingId]` answer 400 (`Wallet holdings come from the chain`) for it; lines typed before stay listed and can only be deleted. Public keys are validated before they are saved (`crypto/wallet-keys/public-key.ts`: base58check, bech32 / bech32m, EIP-55; private keys, WIF, recovery phrases, descriptors and testnet refused, each with its own message; the web form adds « Où trouver ma clé ? » per wallet), unique across accounts, reachable only with the session (`@TokenAccess('none')`), and never logged or sent anywhere but the wallet sync.
- **Wallet sync** (`apps/api/src/crypto/wallet-sync/`, #129, [ADR 0002](../adr/0002-wallet-sync-privacy.md)): opt-in (`WALLET_SYNC_ENABLED`, default `false`: no chain client is even built). Bitcoin addresses are derived on the server (`@scure/bip32`, BIP44 / 49 / 84, receive and change branches, strict gap limit 20, cached in memory) in the key's `address_types` (#248: `ypub` / `zpub` their own; an `xpub` the type chosen by the owner, default native SegWit, since Ledger Live exports `xpub` for every account, or detected on the first sync from the first receive address of each type, `detect_address_types`; keys saved before #248 keep their prefix type; `PATCH /api/accounts/:id/wallet-keys/:keyId` changes an xpub's type), a single address is read as it is, and both are read from Esplora (`ESPLORA_URL`, `BitcoinChainProvider`); EVM native and `balanceOf` token balances through JSON-RPC per chain (`EVM_RPC_URLS` keyed by chain id over the publicnode.com defaults, `eth_chainId` checked, `EvmChainProvider`). Tokens read = "Ajouter un jeton" only (`crypto_wallet_tokens`, `/api/accounts/:id/wallet-tokens`; manual lines no longer make the sync read their asset, #269): found by name (price id, contracts and decimals from CoinGecko `coins/:id`, `TokenContractProvider`) or by contract address and chain (#269: `decimals()`, `symbol()`, `name()` read with `eth_call` through `EvmChainProvider.tokenInfo`, only the contract sent, EIP-55 checked; stored with `chain`, `contract`, `decimals` and the asset id `erc20.<chain id>.<contract>`, `contract-assets.ts`, which is never sent to the price API and whose line, « sans cours », does not hold the account's valuation back). A sync replaces the account's `sync` lines (one per asset and chain, the chain label as location), sets `crypto_synced_at`, records what a complete read found risen or fallen (#373, above), and runs in the live step of a crypto page (`mode=auto`, below; at most every `WALLET_SYNC_INTERVAL_SECONDS`) and on "Synchroniser" (`POST /api/accounts/:id/holdings/sync`, session only, at most every 30 s); each chain is read on its own (#250): a failing chain keeps its last lines (by location) while the others are replaced, sets `crypto_sync_failed_at` (stale), never a 500, and is named in `sync.failedChains` (chain, reason, `retryAt`; kept in memory). A 429 backs the chain's endpoint off in memory (`ProviderBackoff`: the larger of `Retry-After` and 1 min doubled per rate limit in a row, capped at 1 h): no request to it from page loads or "Synchroniser" until it ends (`sync.retryAt`, « Prochain essai possible à … »). **Bitcoin scans are resumable** (#268, `wallet-sync/bitcoin-scan.ts`): `crypto_bitcoin_scans` (per key and address type: `pass_started_at` while a pass runs, `completed_at`) and `crypto_bitcoin_scan_addresses` (branch, index, used, balance in sats, `read_at`; never the address, derived again) keep every address read; a pass reads the used addresses and the gap window after the last used one per branch, skipping what it already read since `pass_started_at` (a pass over 6 h old starts over), stores its reads even when a failure cuts it short, and the chain stays failed (its line kept) until the pass completes; a single address is a scan of one address. The live step (`syncDue`) reuses a scan completed under `WALLET_SYNC_INTERVAL_SECONDS` ago (no request) and waits at most 5 s (`BACKGROUND_SYNC_WAIT_MS`) for the syncs it starts, which go on in the background (`sync.syncing`, the page reading again every 3 s meanwhile) and value their account once done (`onLate`, #269); "Synchroniser" rescans every key and waits, except the keys completed during a Bitcoin read that failed (it resumes). Esplora is paced at one request per second (`ESPLORA_MIN_INTERVAL_MS`, 1000) and a `Retry-After` ≤ 60 s (none: 10 s) pauses the scan once per sync. `WalletKeyDto.scans` gives each type's used addresses after a complete pass (0 on an xpub: wrong type). Only addresses and token contracts are sent; logs carry the chain id, the failure reason and line counts only.
- A **wallet account's page** (#269, reworked in #294 after the owner's « A. Par actif »; `components/crypto/WalletAccount.tsx`, figures in `wallet-assets.ts`) reads what it holds first, one card per row at every width: « Valeur du wallet » (the value once, its change since the previous value (#360: the shared summary, [investments](../../investments.md#the-summary-of-every-investment-account)), one line « Soldes lus il y a … », « Lecture des chaînes… » while « Actualiser » runs, then one line per chain not read with its retry time, prices not refreshed or an asset without a price, never an alert block; the split by asset as one stacked bar, the four largest then « Autres »), « Avoirs » (one row per asset: ETH on Ethereum and Arbitrum is one row whose quantity is summed exactly with `sumQuantities`, value and share; tapping it unfolds its chains, its price and its age, and the « Supprimer » of a line typed by hand before #269; tokens without a price last under « Sans cours »; the origin badge only when the list mixes origins; the 32px badges dropped on the narrowest cards), « Évolution » (`CryptoHistory`: the daily values then today's live value (#366, `historyPoints`), `SegmentedControl` 6 mois / 1 an / Tout only for the ranges that show more, read only: no list of values, nothing to edit), then « Plus d’informations » (#447): « Mouvements » and « Clés et jetons », whose screen lists the keys as settings rows (owner's « Un écran par clé »): each key (« zpub6rFR…GutZYs · 4 adresses utilisées », or « Aucune adresse utilisée en Legacy (1…) : vérifiez le type ») opens its own screen (`WalletKeySheet`: the key with « Copier », its address type with « Changer » for an xpub, its chains, its last scan, « Retirer la clé » unless it is the only one), « Jetons lus » opens the tokens; « Ajouter une clé » is an item of the page's « ⋮ » (#386). A single « Actualiser » page action (`POST .../holdings/sync`, which reads the chains then forces the prices; prices only when the server does not sync), no « Ajouter un avoir » (nor on its Crypto page card, nor in the Crypto page's account picker), no « Coller un historique ». Since #360 the summary is the shared `InvestmentSummary` (« Valeur du wallet », its status lines, then « Investi », « Résultat » and « Sur 1 an »), the split by asset heads « Avoirs », « Évolution » no longer repeats the change over one year, and « Mouvements » is a row of « Plus d’informations » (#447, before: a card before « Évolution »).

## The Crypto page

- The **Crypto** page (#295, `/crypto`, `components/crypto/CryptoPage.tsx`, direction « A. Actifs puis comptes », chosen in #295) is the portfolio, from `GET /api/crypto/portfolio` (`crypto/portfolio.ts` `aggregatePortfolio`: every open crypto account valued like `summary`, its lines summed per price id with exact quantities (`sumDecimals`), the value the sum of the lines' values so it equals the accounts' total, the change over 24 hours (`changeOverDay`), each account's cost of the asset from the replay (#373: `investedCents` the coins of known cost, `unknownQuantity` the others, the gain on the known ones), fiat lines (`FIAT_TICKERS`) left out, contract-only tokens `priceable: false`; the totals' value adds the euros waiting on exchanges (`fiatCents`), their invested amount is the cost of every priced coin of known cost plus those euros, `withoutInvestedCount` / `withoutInvestedSymbols` / `unknown` the coins of unknown cost, `realised` the sales' gains; then the accounts with `updatedAt`: an exchange's last committed export, a wallet's last sync, a manual account's last edited line). One card per row: « Valeur de vos cryptos » (the total, « +… sur 24 h », « Investi … · résultat … (hors SOL) », one status line per thing wrong, the split by asset), « Actifs » (one row per asset, like the dashboard's accounts: name over its quantity (and where, « 2 comptes », when the row has room) on the left, value over the change on the right beside a ⌄ that turns over when open; tapping it unfolds each account holding it as a bar of its share of the asset, linking to its page, then « Investi », « Résultat » and « Cours » as tiles, one per line below 400px of row, owner's « plus visuel », 20 Nov; « Sans cours » last), « Où sont mes cryptos » (each account: name over how it is fed and how fresh, a small icon first saying what happened (export, read, edit), only the day below 400px of row; the value centred beside the chevron; `accountFreshness` in `portfolio-view.ts`: « à importer » / « à mettre à jour » after `EXPORT_STALE_DAYS` (31) for an exchange, « lecture en échec » for a wallet; opening its page; adding one is « Ajouter des cryptos » of the page's « ⋮ », #386). One « Actualiser » page action (`POST /api/crypto/portfolio/refresh`, session only: syncs every open wallet when the server reads wallets, each at most every 30 s, then forces the prices; offered when prices or a wallet can be read) and one « Ajouter » (`AddCryptoSheet`, a full-screen page on phones, a dialog elsewhere, three steps, one decision per screen with « Retour » and « Étape n sur 3 »): 1. « D’où viennent ces cryptos ? » (une plateforme / un wallet / saisie manuelle, a tap moves on); 2. a platform (the exchanges already followed to add an export to, Binance or Coinbase, « Une autre plateforme » whose exports are not read going to the manual entry) then its export (the account is created with the first upload, `previewExport`), or a wallet (Ledger, Trezor, MetaMask, another with its name) then its public keys (`NewWalletKeys`, `WalletSyncOffNotice`), or an asset (`AssetPicker`) then the quantity, the invested amount, a manual price without the price API and « Où sont-elles ? » (a manual account or a new one, named or « Crypto »); 3. the summary (the export's `Preview` and « Importer n lignes », dropping the pending import on « Retour » or close; the wallet's keys masked and « Ajouter le wallet », then its page; the line and « Ajouter »). `HoldingEditor` only adds or edits a line typed by hand on a known account (account pages), in steps (#323): « Actif » (the search alone, its results running down the sheet), « Quantité » (quantity and invested amount, decimal pad), « Détails » (sous-compte, manual price, note); an edit keeps its asset and opens at the quantity, « Supprimer » beside « Suivant » / « Enregistrer » on each of its steps. A manual or exchange account's lines (`HoldingsPanel`, owner's « A. Deux lignes ») take two rows: where the line is held (else the coin's name) and its value, then the quantity and the gain or why there is no value; from 1 000 € without cents (`rowAmount` / `rowGain`); the price age once in the card's description, the price, invested amount and note in the editor; the whole row opens it. Assets are drawn with `AssetBadge` (`components/crypto/AssetBadge.tsx`: the ticker on a disc in the coin's own colour from `asset-colors.ts`, BTC orange, ETH blue…, near-black text on light discs; else its split part's palette colour; 32px in rows inside an `@container`, 24px `sm` in the asset search and the editor's recap), and `splitParts` gives a coin its own colour too, the palette going to the others, so a badge and its part of the split match. `GET /api/crypto/summary` and `POST /api/crypto/valuate` (per account) stay in the API; the web no longer reads them.

## Exchange imports

- **Exchange imports** (#128, `apps/api/src/imports/crypto/`, `POST /api/accounts/:id/crypto-imports` → `POST|GET|DELETE /api/crypto-imports/:id[/commit]`): Binance and Coinbase "Transactions" CSVs, detected from their headers (Binance French or English; Coinbase's preamble with the holder's name and user id is skipped unread, as are notes, remarks and addresses). Same model as bank imports: a pending preview (new / already imported lines, unknown operation types, tickers without a price id, the holdings after the import; dropped at commit like a bank import's, #367), nothing written before the commit, dedupe hash with an occurrence index among identical lines of the file (`dedupe.ts`), bad lines reported. Lines become `crypto_movements` (signed exact quantity, kind, EUR amount when exported); the commit replaces the account's `import` holdings with their sum per price id (`holdings.ts`: fiat left out, `transfer_internal` ignored, renamed tickers merged through `assets.ts`, invested = EUR cost incl. fees when the export gives it, unknown otherwise) and never touches manual lines. Tickers outside `assets.ts` are looked up with the asset search (ticker only) or chosen by the owner, and saved in `crypto_asset_aliases`. Session-only routes. **Withdrawals to the Ledger** (#296, ADR 0004, #373; `imports/crypto/movement-links.ts`): the commit names on new withdrawals and deposits (`counterpart_account_id`) the only open wallet or manual crypto account holding the asset (else it can be named on the exchange page, `PATCH /api/crypto-movements/:id`, session only), then runs the matching of transfers (#373); their costs follow the coins through the replay, never stored. `GET /api/investments/movements[?accountId=&months=]` lists bank transfers to investments and these crypto movements with their cost (« Mouvements » in « Plus d’informations » on crypto account pages, #447; the MCP `savings_and_goals`).

## Ledger Live imports

- **A wallet's own history** (#426, `imports/crypto/parsers/ledger-live.parser.ts`, the same routes and life cycle as the exchange imports: `POST /api/accounts/:id/crypto-imports` on a **wallet** account → pending preview → commit or undo; session only). The Ledger Live operations export (« Paramètres › Comptes › Exporter l’historique des opérations »), detected from its header: comma separated, never quoted, CRLF, `Operation Date` (UTC ISO), `Status`, `Currency Ticker`, `Operation Type`, `Operation Amount` (unsigned, an `OUT` including its fee), `Operation Fees` (empty on a token account), `Operation Hash`, `Account Name`, then `Account xpub` and the countervalues, **never read**. Columns follow Ledger Live's `csvExport.ts`: **to confirm on a real export** (anonymised fixture `LEDGER_LIVE_LINES` in `testing/crypto-fixtures.ts`). Kinds: `IN` deposit, `OUT` withdrawal, `FEES` fee, `REWARD` / `REWARD_PAYOUT` / `WITHDRAW` income, a failed operation or one of the « stake » family its fee only, `NONE` and NFT lines nothing, the other « out » types `other` with their quantity, an unknown type `other` flagged; an `OUT` and an `IN` of one transaction and ticker in the same file (a move between two accounts of the export) are both `transfer_internal`, neither asked about nor matched (the replay leaves them out, so their network fee is not taken from the pool). `Account Name` is kept as the line's `wallet`, left out of the dedupe hash (a renamed account changes nothing), which takes the transaction hash's digest instead (`dedupe.ts`). **Privacy**: of the transaction hash only a SHA-256 is kept (`crypto_movements.tx_hash_digest`, `txHashDigest`: lower case, no `0x`), never the hash, an address or the xpub; nothing from the file is logged.
- **Holdings stay the chain's**: the commit writes movements only (no `import` holdings, the preview projects none, `crypto_synced_at` untouched). **Observed and imported movements never both count** (`CryptoCostBasisService.readPositions` / `reconcileObserved`, in the commit's and the undo's transaction): what the wallet held at each read known (the observed movements' instants and the last read) is taken before the imported movements change, then the observed movements are rewritten so that, per asset and at each read, observed + imported still add up to it: a read fully explained by the operations keeps no observed movement, what they don't explain stays observed; a movement whose quantity must change is replaced (its answer and link with it: the imported operation now carries them), one that stays the same is kept. A read's movements once all replaced are forgotten: undoing an import gives the reads still known back their quantities, the earliest of them opening the account. A wallet's later read (`observe`) compares the chain with its observed **and** imported movements, so it records only what neither explains. The wallet account is what its keys read: an asset of the export the reads never see (a Ledger account whose key is not in the app) is balanced by an observed departure at the reads. The daily values of a wallet add its imported movements to its observed ones (`observedLines`).
- **Matching by transaction hash**: `matchTransfers` first pairs a departure and an arrival of the same asset in two accounts carrying the same digest, the only two of it (certain: no window, fee nor account named needed), then the others by the rules of #373; two sides carrying **different** digests never match. No supported exchange export carries a transaction hash yet, so an exchange withdrawal is matched to the Ledger's deposit by the rules (amount within the fee, 3 days, the account the withdrawal names), or by hash once an exchange format gives one. In the replay, a wallet's imported arrivals take the place of the account-level links of #296: a withdrawal naming the wallet carries its cost there only before the wallet's first read or first imported movement of the asset. « À préciser » asks the wallet's imported deposits and withdrawals not linked (an arrival from nowhere known, a payment out); `GET /api/investments/movements` lists a transfer once (an imported deposit linked to a withdrawal is that withdrawal), and only an exchange's lines can be named by hand. Web: « Importer l’historique Ledger Live » in the wallet page's « ⋮ » opens `ExchangeImportSheet` (`source="wallet"`), whose preview says the holdings stay the chain's.
