# Month-end review (closing a month)

Feature details moved out of [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md) (#422). The domain rules, workflow conventions and architecture there apply here too.

- The guided « Bilan du mois » (`/month-review`, `apps/api/src/month-close/`, `GET|PATCH /api/month-close/:month`) computes its steps from existing data: imports up to date per cash account (the month review's missing accounts, missing days, running-balance gaps from the stored bank rows, manual transactions excluded), the month's review queue, recurring items (no `suggested` left; the month's price changes and cancelled items charged during the month on or after their end date, `chargedAfterEnd`, ticked as seen), budget and savings, investment values at the month end (crypto accounts never: computed, #366), then the close. Only `recurring` is ticked by hand (`month_closes.acknowledged`, « Vu » in its sheet); `budget` and `savings` are always done since #541 (the page shows them, nothing to tick), the enum and older ticks are kept for the MCP and stored rows.
- **The page** (#541, the owner's « La réponse d'abord », one column on every screen): the month's answer first (`MonthAnswer`: saved and the savings rate, the savings target, income and expenses as two bars of one scale with the planned income as a notch, the expenses against the month before, the budgeted categories' spending against their targets), all read from the report (`closedReport ?? report`), so a closed month shows its frozen figures; then « À régler »: only the steps left as round orange icons with their count (`MonthFixes`, `fixesLeft`), each opening its detail in a sheet, or going straight to Opérations' review filter or Abonnements when there is nothing to read first, « Tout est en ordre » when none is left; then the five largest expense categories against the month before, « Tout voir » opening the whole report (`MonthReport`, its CSV) in a sheet. The bottom bar: ✎ (the month's note, in a sheet) and « Clôturer » (one tap when nothing is left, « Clôturer quand même ? » otherwise), « Rouvrir » once closed; a closed month whose figures moved shows « Modifié depuis la clôture » (the changes in a sheet).
- `POST /api/month-close/:month/close` (months before the current Europe/Paris month only) freezes the report (flow, net worth at the month end, savings target, totals per category with the month before and targets, largest one-offs, notes) in `month_closes.report` with its shape's version (`report_version`, #369: `REPORT_VERSION` in `month-close/report-version.ts`, the database refusing one without the other); later edits show as `changes` against the live report. A frozen report is read only through `upgradeReport(version, json)`, which turns any stored version into the current DTO one step at a time, filling what an older shape lacks and never recomputing a figure (1 → 2: categories without an icon take their current one, reports before #315 get `plan: null`); a change of what the report holds or means bumps the version and adds one step, so months closed before keep their totals. A closed month refuses ticks and notes until `POST .../reopen`, which drops the frozen report and its version and keeps the notes. Closing is open to write tokens (`@TokenAccess('write')`, #342, the MCP's `close_months`); reopening, ticking steps and notes stay session-only, so undoing a close is done in the app. `GET /api/month-close` lists the closed months, oldest first.
- **Cohérence des chiffres** (#427, `apps/api/src/integrity/`, `GET /api/integrity?month=`, read only, open to read tokens): checks computed from the stored data, one step of the review read by its own query (`integrityQuery`, `components/month-close/IntegrityStep.tsx`); once its checks have answered, a finding (or a failed load) is one more icon in « À régler » (the close asks « Clôturer quand même ? », never refuses), its sheet listing the findings or saying the load failed. The checks, each finding with its account (a link to its page) and day: **balances** (month; bank accounts): the balance recomputed from the bank rows (bank values) vs the bank's running balance (`balance_gap`, `balance-chain.ts`: from each known end-of-day balance, the next one; ambiguous days restart the chain; the month before opens it) and vs the statement balances (`statement_gap`), and the hand-entered transactions dated more than 5 days (the reconciliation window) before the account's data end, never matched (`manual_not_reconciled`: in the totals, not in the balance); **crypto** (now; wallets and manual accounts): each asset's quantity held (a wallet's `sync` lines, a manual account's typed lines) vs the sum of its observed movements, i.e. the last complete chain read or line edit (#373) (`holding_differs_from_read`, dated by `crypto_observed_at`); **owed** (now; read through `OwedService.settlementFigures` / `repaymentsOverTransfer`, so how let-go amounts are stored stays there): share − repaid − let go never below zero (`owed_negative`), the repayments linked to a transfer never past it (`repayment_over_transfer`); **totals** (month): income − expenses from the lines summed per category vs the saved figure of the transactions summary (`flow_mismatch`, `flowMismatch`), and an income or expense of a transactional account mirrored by a transfer (not linked elsewhere) or the opposite kind on another own account, opposite amount, at most 3 days apart (`internal_move_counted`). A check that throws is `failed` and the others still answer; the log line carries the month and the counts per check only. Nothing is fixed automatically. Envelopes vs balances is added with M13 (noted in `integrity.service.ts`). MCP: `integrity_checks`.
