## Month comparisons and month navigation

- A partial month is compared **like for like**: the same first days of the previous month and of the averaged months. An empty month is not compared at all. Without a `month` parameter, endpoints show the current month when it has data, else the month the data stops in. Responses carry a `period` (`MonthPeriodDto`) and the web states it with `PeriodNote` (`lib/period.ts`). ## End-of-month forecast

- The end-of-month forecast (`apps/api/src/forecast/`, `GET /api/forecast`, #215) adds the month's repayments still expected from recurring items shared with a contact (#359, `expectedRepaymentCredits`: what is left of each, a credit on the item's account on the contact's day or the month end, `repaymentFrom` names them; never twice once booked and linked; one-off open receivables and debts are not projected). It projects from the day after `knownUntil` (the selected accounts' `dataUntil`, the latest of them), never from today: recurring items (none on or after a cancelled item's `ended_on`) and the day-to-day estimate fall on their own dates, and the day-to-day expenses entered by hand since then (already in the balance) replace the estimate of the elapsed days (`coveredCents`), so nothing counts twice. Data more than `FORECAST_STALE_AFTER_DAYS` (7) days old (or none) makes it `stale`: indicative, `belowThreshold` null (no low-balance alarm), and the page offers « Importer un relevé » / « Ajouter une dépense ».
