# Month comparisons, forecast and dashboard

Feature details moved out of [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md) (#422). The domain rules, workflow conventions and architecture there apply here too.

## Month comparisons and month navigation

- A partial month is compared **like for like**: the same first days of the previous month and of the averaged months. An empty month is not compared at all. Without a `month` parameter, endpoints show the current month when it has data, else the month the data stops in. Responses carry a `period` (`MonthPeriodDto`) and the web states it with `PeriodNote` (`lib/period.ts`). Every month page (Mouvements, Opérations, Budget, Épargne, Bilan du mois; not the dashboard, which follows today since #405) moves between months with the shared `MonthNavigator` (`components/month/`, #213): previous / next, ← / → on a keyboard (no swipe since #446: the owner found it unclear; on a phone a sideways swipe opens the sidebar), the month name opening a picker with the months that have transactions marked and shortcuts to the current month and to the month the data stops in, from `GET /api/coverage` (`dataUntil`, `defaultMonth`, `months`). **The month is part of the app's frame** (#401, the owner's « B. ## End-of-month forecast

- The end-of-month forecast (`apps/api/src/forecast/`, `GET /api/forecast`, #215) adds the month's repayments still expected from recurring items shared with a contact (#359, `expectedRepaymentCredits`: what is left of each, a credit on the item's account on the contact's day or the month end, `repaymentFrom` names them; never twice once booked and linked; one-off open receivables and debts are not projected). It projects from the day after `knownUntil` (the selected accounts' `dataUntil`, the latest of them), never from today: recurring items (none on or after a cancelled item's `ended_on`) and the day-to-day estimate fall on their own dates, and the day-to-day expenses entered by hand since then (already in the balance) replace the estimate of the elapsed days (`coveredCents`), so nothing counts twice. Data more than `FORECAST_STALE_AFTER_DAYS` (7) days old (or none) makes it `stale`: indicative, `belowThreshold` null (no low-balance alarm), and the page offers « Importer un relevé » / « Ajouter une dépense ».

## Dashboard

- **The dashboard is a quick daily glance** (#405, the owner's direction « A. À faire, puis un chiffre », replacing the monthly review of #262 / #321): the first screen when the app opens, answering in order « is there anything to do? », « how much can I still spend this month? », « do I end the month in the green? » and « where does my net worth stand? ». It **follows today, not a month**: no month bar, no `?month=` (an old link's is dropped), past months are read in « Bilan du mois »; the title's description is today's date (« Samedi 21 novembre »). Top to bottom (`components/dashboard/`): **« À faire »** (`DashboardTodos`, hidden when all is done; logic in `glance.ts` `buildTodos`, tested): a statement to import when the bank data (`GET /coverage` `dataUntil`) is more than `STALE_AFTER_DAYS` (7) days old or missing, last month to close (« Clôturer octobre », with the accounts to update before, from `GET /api/dashboard/review?month=` of the previous month, only when that month has data), the current month's budget to prepare once it is closed (`nextMonthBudgeted`), the review queue, repayments to link (`GET /api/receivables/suggestions`), prepaid renewals (`renewalReminder`), detected subscriptions (`suggested`) and investment values to update (`stale`), each a row opening its page; past `TODOS_SHOWN` (3) the rest behind « n autres » in the card's header, so the figures stay on the first screen. Then **one card** (`GlanceCard`): « Reste à dépenser en novembre » in large (target − spent in the budgeted categories of the current month's review, « Budget dépassé » in red once over), a tap opening Budget, against an even pace **read up to the day the data goes to** (`readDay`: `throughDay` while the month is partial, like for like; `budgetPace`: « 378 € sous le rythme », « Dans le rythme » within 10 €, « 75 € au-dessus du rythme » in the warning tone, « Jour 28 sur 30 » once over), a bar with the pace mark, « 891 € sur 2 225 € · 88 € hors budget » and « À jour au 19 nov. » (« Chiffres au 11 nov. » in the warning tone once stale), or « Pas de budget pour novembre » with « Fixer un budget »; then two rows (`GlanceRow`): « Fin de mois » (`ForecastRow`, the forecast's end balance, its low point or « Sous 500 € le 29 nov. » on a warning tint, « · indicatif » when stale, to `/forecast`) and « Patrimoine » (today's net worth and « +1 240 € depuis le 31 oct. », `previousMonthEndCents`, to Comptes). Then **« Enveloppes »** (`MonthEnvelopes`, the current month's tracked budget lines, `GET /api/budgets/envelopes`): one line each, the whole line a button that notes an expense in it (owner's « Appuyer = ajouter »): category icon, name, what's left in whole euros (amber when ahead of the pace, « −12 € » red once over), a thin bar with the pace mark, a quiet 32px « + » (primary text on a light tint, the owner's request: a filled one took too much room); « Budget › » opens the detail; « Ajouter une dépense » is the page's only « ⋮ » item (#386). What left the dashboard is one tap away on its page: the accounts and the two parts of the net worth on Comptes, the month's income / expenses / saved and the net worth at a month end on Bilan du mois (its report) and Épargne, the budget's split with / without a budget on Budget, money owed on Dépenses partagées (sidebar badge), and the 12-month « Revenus et dépenses » chart on Épargne (`savings/CashFlowChart.tsx`, at the end of the page). `GET /api/dashboard/review` keeps serving the MCP's `month_review` and the dashboard's two months.
