# Transactions

Feature details moved out of [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md) (#422). The domain rules, workflow conventions and architecture there apply here too.

## Kind and transfer links

- **A categorized transaction or split part has its category's kind, always** (#368, [ADR 0005](../adr/0005-data-model-consolidation.md) R1): an incoming amount in an expense category is a **refund** of it (it nets its spending, budget and envelope), an outgoing one in an income category lowers that income, split or not. `transactions.kind` is chosen freely only on an uncategorized row (review queue); otherwise it is a copy the `transactions_kind_follows_category` trigger (`BEFORE INSERT OR UPDATE OF category_id, kind`) keeps equal to the category's on every write path, dropping a transfer link or a prepaid link the new kind refuses (the #367 check constraints forbid them). `transaction_lines.kind` is `coalesce(line's category kind, t.kind)`. `PATCH /api/transactions/:id`, `bulk-update` and manual transactions answer 400 for a `kind` other than the given category's, and a kind sent alone is refused on a row filed in a category of another kind (`transactions/category-kind.ts`). Rules have no kind of their own (`categorization_rules.kind` dropped): a rule filing transfers files them in a transfer category. To change the kind of a categorized row, choose a category of that kind: the editor's « Type › » does so (« Classer en revenu » lists the categories of that kind, or « Sans catégorie, à revoir »), the bulk « Changer le type… » is only offered when no selected row has a category. Migration `0040` put the rows that disagreed in review with their category's kind and printed only their count (`finances: …` notices are printed by `database/migrate.ts`).
- **Transfer links** (#296, [ADR 0004](../adr/0004-transfer-links-and-contributions.md)): `transactions.counterpart_account_id` names the owner's other account a transfer goes to (amount < 0) or comes from (amount > 0); only on transfers (any change of kind away from `transfer` clears it, the API answers 400 otherwise), never the row's own account (check constraint), `ON DELETE SET NULL`. Set by hand (`PATCH /api/transactions/:id` `counterpartAccountId`; « Vers » / « Depuis » in the transaction editor's « Options », shown on the row), by a rule's « Compte de destination » (`categorization_rules.counterpart_account_id`, rules filing transfers only, given to the transfers they file that have none, on import and on `apply`; an earlier link is never overwritten) and by matching (`transactions/transfer-links/`: the two sides of a transfer between two open cash accounts, opposite amounts at most 3 days apart, closest wins, a tie between two accounts links nothing; both sides linked) on the rows an import (bank CSV, history, demo) just inserted, so a link removed by hand stays removed. `GET /transactions?counterpartAccountId=` lists them. A reconciled manual transfer passes its link to the bank row.

## Manual transactions

- **Manual transactions** (`POST|PATCH|DELETE /api/transactions/manual`, `ManualTransactionsService`, #137 then #74) are transactions of any kind (expense, income, transfer) with `manual = true`, no import and a random `manual:<uuid>` dedupe hash: positive `amountCents` + `direction` (`out`/`in`, default `in` for income), optional category (without one: review queue, label required), day (never in the future), label, account (open checking / savings / meal voucher; default the oldest open checking), one-off flag, note. They count in statistics and budgets at once, and in account balances from the day after the bank anchor (they never date the balance), but never as imported data: `coverage/` (`dataUntil`) ignores them and the import dedupe (`loadExistingForRange`) leaves them out. The web enters them with `ManualTransactionSheet` (the pages' « Ajouter une dépense / un revenu / un virement », dashboard envelopes), which is also the editor of every transaction (#398, [hand edits](#hand-edits-74)). The sheet goes in four steps (#245 then #398, owner's choice: one decision per screen, the same steps to add and to edit): **Catégorie** (the category picker, [web conventions](../../web-conventions.md#mobile-layout-conventions), for the kind chosen before adding: no kind tiles, #398; « Habituels » are the usual labels, a tap filling the label and its category; a tap moves on), **Montant** (the sign before the amount, the kind's own, a tap for the other one: a refund of an expense, a returned income, a transfer into or out of the account, said under the amount), **Détails** (only the day and the account: the day on `DayStrip`, `components/dates/DayStrip.tsx` (also « Quelqu'un a payé pour moi »'s day, #398), the owner's « bande de jours »: whole days only, 5 to 7 as fit (`daysThatFit`), the oldest on the left and today at the end, « Auj. », the month on a 1st, ‹ › above it or a swipe turning the page, its calendar button opening the month in place of everything else of the step (`Calendar` of `@capi/ui`, French; ‹ › then the month's name and ✕ on one line; every week drawn, the first starting with the month before, the days to come faded and « Les jours à venir ne se choisissent pas »; round days, large figures, the weeks sharing the height; at most 420px wide, nothing to scroll), the chosen day named above; the account as rows, `AccountRows` in `manual-fields.tsx`: the type's badge, the name and the balance, the meal-voucher card only for restaurants and groceries or once chosen; for an expense « Payé par », ending with « Quelqu’un d’autre », which opens « Qui a payé ? » in the same frame; for a transfer « Depuis » (the cash accounts) and « Vers » (every other open account but a crypto or meal-voucher one), « Ajouter » making the transfer out of the first linked to the second (`counterpartAccountId`) and, when « Vers » is a cash account, its side coming in, linked back, one « Annuler » deleting both), **Options** (the label, then for an expense « Partage »: « J'ai payé pour quelqu'un › » (`ShareFields` of `owed/ShareScreen.tsx`, who owes the owner what) or, when someone else paid, « Je dois à Camille › » (`MyPartFields` of `owed/PaidForMeSheet.tsx`: how many we were, my part), kept as a draft until « Ajouter », which creates the expense and its shares, or the « Quelqu'un a payé pour moi » debt of my part (`POST /api/receivables/debts`), with one « Annuler »; then Exceptionnelle, the note). Adding moves on with « Suivant » on every step and adds on « Options » only (the owner's request); a transfer has no sign to choose (its accounts say it). An envelope opens at the amount. The kind is chosen before adding (#398, the owner's choice): the pages offer « Ajouter une dépense », « Ajouter un revenu » and « Ajouter un virement » (Mouvements, Opérations, a cash account's page; the envelopes and the forecast add expenses, the meal-voucher card a payment or a credit). Usual labels come from `GET /api/transactions/manual/suggestions?kind=` (labels typed on manual transactions over a year, counted double, and merchants of imported rows seen twice in six months, each with the category filed ≥ 70 % of the time; plus the kind's most used categories over 90 days, the first being the default, #196). On phones (`useIsMobile`) it is a full-screen editor (#385, [web conventions](../../web-conventions.md#mobile-layout-conventions)): the round bar on top (✕ or ‹, the four steps, › « Suivant » then ✓ « Ajouter »), the amount typed in its field with the phone's decimal keyboard (exact text, never a float; the app's own keypad of #245 was dropped by the owner in #385), focused when the amount step opens. Elsewhere a dialog: the amount is typed in its field (or anywhere on the step, `amount-entry.ts`) and Enter moves on, then adds. The directions considered and the owner's choice are in #245. Every control is 44px high. Date inputs drop their native look and sheets clip horizontal overflow (`packages/ui` `Input` / `Sheet`), so nothing pans sideways on iOS.

## Hand edits (#74)

- **Imported rows** stay editable for category, kind, flags and note (`PATCH /api/transactions/:id`). Their amount and date are the bank's and never change (#398, the owner's decision): no correction route any more, and the `transactions_bank_values_kept` trigger (migration `0045`) refuses any update of a bank row's amount or date but putting back the bank's values of a row corrected before; `DELETE /api/transactions/:id/correction` (« Rétablir les valeurs de la banque » in the editor's options of such a row, no « Annuler ») is all that is left of « Modifier une opération importée ». The bank values of a row corrected before #398 are kept in `original_amount_cents` / `original_booking_date` (imported rows only, a check constraint) and read through the generated stored columns `bank_amount_cents` / `bank_booking_date` (#367; `bankAmountCents` / `bankBookingDate` in `transactions.schema.ts`, never written; index `(account_id, bank_booking_date) where not manual`, so bank reads filter `manual = false`) by everything that is bank data (balances, continuity checks, `coverage/`, the dedupe of later imports, month-close gaps), so a re-import never duplicates or overwrites a correction and the dedupe hash never changes. Statistics use the corrected values.
- **Splits** (`transaction_splits`, `PUT|DELETE /api/transactions/:id/splits`): 2–20 parts, each with the transaction's sign, summing exactly to its amount in cents; the transaction keeps the category of its largest part for display. Per-category statistics (spending, budgets and envelopes, planned income, month-close totals, savings flows, the dashboard's monthly flows, the forecast's day-to-day estimate, `GET /transactions/summary`) read the `transaction_lines` view (one line per part, else the transaction), so a split counts in each category. **A part counts with its category's kind** (#357, the general rule since #368; the view reads the category's kind, nothing is stored on the part, since a category's kind can't change while used and a deleted category's parts move to one of the same kind): an incoming part in an expense category is a **refund** of that category (it nets its spending, budget and envelope), an incoming part in an income category is income, an outgoing part in an income category lowers it. The transaction takes its largest part's category and with it its kind; a transfer category beside income or expense ones is refused (400). The DTO gives each part its `kind`; « Répartir » (`SplitEditor`) labels a refund part « Remboursement : déduit des dépenses … ». `PUT` is open to write tokens (MCP `split_transaction`), `DELETE` stays session-only. Frozen month-close reports keep their totals; a later split shows in `changes`. A category set for the whole transaction ends its split; a split transaction's amount is locked until the split is removed.
- **Web**: a transactions row (#266, `TransactionRow.tsx`) is the category's icon in its colour (`CategoryAvatar`, a check when selected: tapping it selects the row, a warning dot when to review), one label (`displayLabel`; the raw bank label under it only when it says more, `secondaryLabel`), the category, the account only when there are several open accounts and the list is not filtered to one, small flag icons with an accessible name (exceptionnelle, saisie à la main, corrigée, note; « À revoir » is the only text badge) and the amount (`Amount`: income green with « + », expenses in the text colour, internal transfers muted after ⇄; red is kept for what is wrong). Phones stack it on two lines; wide pages (`@xl` container) put the category (`CategoryMenu`: its name as text, opening the categories by kind) and the amount in columns, the account from `@2xl`, with no column headers. On wide pages the category and the amount of manual rows are edited in place. Activating the label opens the row's editor (#398, the owner's direction: adding and editing in one shape): `ManualTransactionSheet` with the row's `item` and its `actions` (`TransactionEditActions`, `components/transactions/edit-options.tsx`; a full-screen page on phones, a centred dialog elsewhere), the same four steps filled in, opened on « Options » (on « Catégorie » for a row without one, the owner's request), every step a tap away. **One draft, one save** (#435, the owner's option B): the steps and every screen of the options (category, kind, flags, note, the other account, split, shares, repayments, prepaid, the bank values) change the editor's draft only, nothing is saved while editing; « Modifications non enregistrées » shows under the bar on every step and screen while something changed, and ✕ (or Escape, or « Je dois à … » leaving for the contact's page) asks « Abandonner les modifications ? » first, « Abandonner » closing it as it was (the next opening starts from the transaction again). ✓ « Enregistrer » (greyed out until something changed) sends only what changed in one request, `POST /api/transactions/:id/edit` (`transactions/edit/`, `TransactionEditService`, session only): `manual` (a manual row's fields), `changes` (category, kind, flags, note, `counterpartAccountId`), `split` (parts, or `null` to end it), `shares`, `prepaidTermMonths`, `envelope` (#430, [`envelopes.md`](envelopes.md)), `repayments` (`unlink` payment ids of this transaction, `link` items) and `restoreBankValues`, each through the service of its own route (its rules and refusals), all in **one database transaction**: the services are built on it (`DatabaseService.within(tx)`, their own transactions becoming savepoints), so a refusal anywhere leaves the transaction, its split, shares, repayments and prepaid item as they were (`transaction-edit.service.spec.ts`, one failure forced). The order lets one save go from one state to another: unlinks, shares lowered or dropped, a prepaid item made ordinary and an ended split first, then the manual fields and the changes, then a new split, a prepaid term, shares raised or added and new links. The option screens change the draft as they are used, with nothing to validate on them: « Répartir » keeps its parts as typed (‹ keeps them, « Réinitialiser » puts back the parts as the editor opened, « Ne plus répartir » ends a split it had); a split that does not add up shows « À compléter » on its row, and ✓ opens its screen with what is wrong, saving nothing. The web builds the body with `editBody` and its undo with `undoBody` (`components/transactions/edit-draft.ts`): the toast « Annuler » sends the save that puts it back, except after the bank values were put back or a prepaid item was declared from it. A new category still offers its rule. The quick « Classer » (#322), the category and amount picked in a wide row, bulk edits, manual entry and the MCP keep their own routes. A bank row's category, flags and note only: its line of steps has only « Catégorie » and « Options » (nothing greyed out that would look like a step), its amount, day, label and account the bank's, said once under the amount with a lock (#398); one corrected before #398 shows the bank's values and « Rétablir les valeurs de la banque » in its options (in the draft: « Garder la correction » takes it back). The type is a category of that type (#368): « Catégorie » lists the row's kind and ends with « Un autre type » (« Classer en revenu », « Classer en virement »…), which lists that kind's categories, or « Sans catégorie, à revoir » with that kind. Its « Options » add « Cette opération » (`EditOptionRows`): « Vers » / « Depuis » (a transfer's other account), Partager, Je dois à …, « On me rembourse » (money in) / « Je rembourse » (money out), each only when something is owed that way or already linked, Répartir (« Répartition » once split), Prépayé, each its own screen in the same frame (‹ « Retour aux options »); then « À revoir » beside Exceptionnelle, and for a manual row « Supprimer l’opération » (deleted at once, after its confirmation). A split row's category step leads to its split, its amount is locked. « Classer » (#322) opens on « Catégorie » and a tap saves the category at once. The editor has no keyboard shortcuts (#265) beyond Enter: its controls are reached with Tab and used like any button. Every edit shows a toast with "Annuler" (`use-transaction-actions.ts`; the editor's save one for all it changed).
