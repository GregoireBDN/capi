# Envelopes

Money set aside for a goal (#429, [ADR 0008](../adr/0008-envelopes.md), decisions on #428): a trip, an item to buy, a deposit. The domain rules, workflow conventions and architecture of [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md) apply here too. Screens: #430 (below).

- **Never a transaction.** An envelope's amount on an account is the sum of its `envelope_movements` there; nothing that reads balances, net worth, statistics, the month report or the forecast reads them. Net worth is identical before and after any envelope change (`envelopes.service.spec.ts`).
- **An envelope** (`envelopes`): name, icon (a web icon name), colour, optional target (`target_cents` > 0) and due date, optional expense categories (`envelope_categories`, offers only), archived. `GET /api/envelopes[?includeArchived=true]` (by due date, none last) and `GET /api/envelopes/:id` give its total, `paidCents` (what it paid, refunds deducted), `putAsideCents` (total + paid), its amount per account and `plan`: `remainingCents`, `monthsLeft` (month ends until the due date, as goals), `requiredMonthlyCents` (« 221 € par mois pendant 7 mois »), `targetDatePassed`. The detail adds « Évolution » (`history`: the last 12 month ends, per account) and its latest 200 movements. `POST /api/envelopes` (with `start`: an amount per account taken from « libre », 400 beyond it), `PATCH` (`categoryIds` replaces them, `archived`), `DELETE` (its ledger goes: the money is « libre » again).
- **An account's split** (`GET /api/accounts/:id/envelopes`): its envelopes (largest first, `shareOfBalance` in basis points: « 22 % du solde »), `allocatedCents`, « libre » (`freeCents` = balance − envelopes, negative when the balance falls under them) and `shortfallCents` (« Il manque 150 € pour les couvrir »), reported and **never reduced automatically**: the owner chooses with moves; money coming back clears it by itself. An envelope below zero on an account only happens after an import was undone or an account deleted (the transfer that brought it is gone); it shows as a negative amount, never refused.
- **Moves** (`POST /api/envelopes/moves`): on one account, from an envelope or « libre » (null) to another or « libre »; never more than the source holds there (400), never into an archived envelope. A move between two envelopes is two rows naming each other (`counter_envelope_id`). The balance does not change.
- **Split key** (`PUT /api/accounts/:id/envelope-key`, the whole key): basis points per envelope (1–10 000, at most 10 000 in all, the rest « libre »; archived envelopes take none). Kept rows keep their creation time, which dates the key.
- **Offers** (`GET /api/envelopes/offers`), nothing attached without the owner:
  - `savings`: an incoming income or transfer row created after the account's key, not split, not answered, and not the arriving side of a transfer that already carries an envelope (same amount, linked back, `TRANSFER_MATCH_TOLERANCE_DAYS` apart), with each envelope's share by the key (rounded down to the cent). Confirmed with `POST /api/envelopes/splits/:transactionId` (the key, or `shares` given, at most the saving), once.
  - `transfers`: a transfer out towards a **checking** account (#430, « je rapatrie pour payer »; a move to savings or an investment is not offered, it can still be attached in the transaction editor), with the envelopes holding money on its account that existed on its booking day (most first): like expenses, counted from each envelope's creation (the owner, 26 Nov), so attaching an old transfer brings no older ones.
  - `expenses`: an expense in an envelope's categories (any of its lines), booked since the envelope was created (Paris day), on an account where it holds money.
  - « Non »: `POST /api/envelopes/offers/:transactionId/dismiss` (`envelope_offer_dismissals`), for every offer on that transaction.
- **Attaching** (`POST /api/envelopes/:id/transactions`, one envelope per transaction, 409 otherwise):
  - an **expense** keeps its category and counts once in the month; the envelope pays it (`spend`, on the expense's account): by default all of the owner's part (`transaction_lines`), at most what it holds on that account; `amountCents` pays part of it (« 1 000 € de l'ordinateur, le reste sur le budget »). A refund attached gives back. What it paid is attributed to the expense's lines in order (`envelopes/paid.ts`) and **adds to that month's budget** of those categories ([`budgets.md`](budgets.md)). `GET /api/envelopes/month?month=` lists the envelopes that paid in a month, paid per category, put aside and what is left (« Il reste 85 € » → a move to « libre » or another envelope).
  - a **transfer out** (with its counterpart set) carries the envelope's money to the other account: two `transfer` legs summing to 0 (« Livret A 300 € → 0 €, Compte courant 400 € → 700 € »), by default all it holds on the source, at most the transfer.
  - `DELETE /api/envelopes/:id/transactions/:transactionId` detaches an expense, a transfer or a split; 409 when the envelope would fall below zero on an account (the money was moved or spent since).
- **After the due date** `plan.targetDatePassed`; what is left goes back by a move. Nothing moves by itself.
- **Write paths** (ADR 0008 §9, §11): the triggers check every movement against its transaction; `transactions_clear_envelope_movements` deletes what a transaction's new kind, account, counterpart or amount forbids (an attached expense refiled as a move between own accounts pays nothing any more). Deleting a transaction deletes its movements (cascade); reconciliation moves them from the manual expense to its bank row and undoing an import back (`imports/undo/`); deleting an account deletes its key, its movements and both legs of the transfers that touched it (counted in the deletion preview, `envelopeMovements`).
- **Access**: tokens and the MCP read (`savings_and_goals` lists each envelope: total, per account, paid, due date, per month needed); every write is session only.
- **Savings goals became envelopes** (#430, the owner's choice, 26 Nov): `db:seed`, run on every deploy after the migrations, calls `convertAndRemoveSavingsGoals` (`envelopes/convert-goals.ts`) in one database transaction: same id, name, colour, target, due date and archive state; an `accounts` goal takes what its linked accounts still have « libre » up to its target, nearest due date first; a `manual` goal's amount goes on the bank account with the most « libre », what none can hold is reported (`unplacedCents`, the seed logs the count only); then the goals are deleted, so an envelope deleted later never comes back. The goals routes and screens are gone; the `savings_goals` tables stay until a later migration drops them. The month close's savings step lists envelopes (`CloseSavingsDto.envelopes`; reports frozen before keep their goals).
- **Transaction editor** (#435): `TransactionDto` carries `envelopeId` and `envelopeAmountCents` (the `spend` or `transfer` movement); the editor's « Enveloppe › » option (an expense out, or a transfer out with its counterpart) picks one with what it holds on the account, or « Non », and an amount (empty: all it can); `POST /api/transactions/:id/edit` takes `envelope` (`{ envelopeId, amountCents? }` or `null`) and detaches the old one before the split changes and attaches the new one after the fields, in the same database transaction.

## Screens (#430)

The owner's directions on #428; `apps/web/src/components/envelopes/`. Few words, each envelope its icon and colour, « libre » hatched.

- **A bank account's page** (« A. Barre et liste », `AccountEnvelopesCard`, checking and savings only): the balance as one stacked bar (each envelope in its colour, « libre » hatched, what is missing hatched red), a row per envelope with « 37 % du solde » opening its page, « Libre » last, « Déplacer » and « + Nouvelle enveloppe ». When the balance is under its envelopes: « Solde sous vos enveloppes · Il manque X » with « Choisir quoi réduire » (`ReduceSheet`: − / + by 10 €, the last step snapping to what is left; « Réduire » enabled only once exactly the missing amount is chosen, made as moves to « libre ») and « Plus tard » (hidden for the session). « Clé de répartition » (`SplitKeySheet`): − / + by 5 % per envelope, « Libre » the rest, « Pour 300 € épargnés » as an example.
- **An envelope's page** (`/envelopes/:id`, « A. Jauge », `EnvelopePage`): icon, « 1 080 € sur 1 800 € », the gauge, « 103 € par mois pendant 7 mois » (or « Il manque … », « Date passée »), « A payé 420 € », « Ajouter » and « Retirer » as two round buttons, a filled « + » and a light « − » in its colour (« A. Ronds »; `AddWithdrawSheet`, the owner's « simplifier »: an amount, on the account chosen by itself, the most « libre » to add, the most held to withdraw, « Autre compte » to change it; a move between envelopes stays on the account's page), « Modifier » in « ⋮ » (`EnvelopeEditor`: name, icon, colour, target, date, « Paie pour » categories, archive, delete); « Sur Livret A » when it is on one account, else « Par compte » (one bar, a line per account), « Évolution » (once it held money) (the 12 month ends stacked per account), « Paie pour ».
- **Creating** (« B. Modèle d'abord », `CreateEnvelopeSheet`, from Épargne's « Nouvelle enveloppe » or an account's card), six steps: a template (Voyage, Un achat, Un apport, Imprévus, Cadeaux, Autre: icon, colour, categories), the name and icon, « Un objectif ? » (two tiles, « Un objectif » with its amount or « Sans objectif », the owner: « Pas de montant » read oddly), « Pour quand ? » (a row per choice, 3, 6 or 12 months, a date, none, each with what it takes per month; skipped without a target), « Déjà de côté ? » (one amount taken from « libre », like « Ajouter »: on the account it was started from, else the one with the most « libre », « Autre compte » to change it; empty = nothing yet), « C'est bon ? » (a recap whose rows go back to their step). It opens the new envelope's page.
- **Déplacer** (`MoveSheet`, an account's page): « De », « Vers », the amount with « Tout ».
- **Épargne**: « Enveloppes » (`EnvelopesSection`, replacing « Objectifs ») lists the active ones with their gauge, « Voir les archivées » last, the empty state « Mettre de l'argent de côté pour un projet ». « À ranger » (`OffersCard`) above it and at the end of Opérations' review queue: savings to split by the key (one button with each share), transfers and expenses with one button per envelope, « Non »; two shown, then « Voir les n autres ».
- **Budget** (« C. Une ligne Enveloppe », `EnvelopeMonthLines`): an envelope that paid this month is a line « 715 € / 800 € » (paid / put aside) on a gauge in its colour, opening to what it paid per category and « Il reste 85 € » with « Rendre à « libre » » or « Vers une autre enveloppe ». A category line shows « + 95 € par Lisbonne » and its own share; the total « Dépensé … · 715 € par une enveloppe ».
