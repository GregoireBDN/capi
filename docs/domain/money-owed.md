# Money owed (Dépenses partagées, #359)

Feature details moved out of [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md) (#422). The domain rules, workflow conventions and architecture there apply here too.

- **Contacts** (`contacts`, `owed/contacts.schema.ts`: name unique case ignored, `match_labels` (words of their bank labels), `repayment_day`, `archived_at`): anyone the owner shares expenses with; no account, no sign-in. Created on the fly from a name (`contactName` wherever a contact is taken), managed in Réglages › Contacts; a contact with history is archived, never deleted (409). Names, labels and amounts are never logged.
- **Receivables** (`receivables`, `owed/owed.schema.ts`), one per expense and person, `direction` `owed_to_me` (the owner advanced the money: a share of an outgoing expense of one of his accounts, at most the expense with the other shares) or `i_owe` (« Quelqu'un a payé pour moi »: a contact paid; the owner's part is a manual expense **without an account**, `transactions.paid_by_contact_id`, check `(account_id is null) = (paid_by_contact_id is not null)`, always a manual expense, with its `i_owe` receivable of that part and the bill in `bill_cents`). A split expense's share names its part (`split_id`, required then); splitting, unsplitting or recategorizing the whole of a transaction that money owed hangs on is refused (400), as is changing the kind of a shared expense or an expense paid by a contact. State is computed, never stored: what is left is the share minus what was repaid and let go; `open` (« à rembourser ») while something is left, else `forgiven` (« abandonné ») if some was let go, else `repaid` (« remboursé »); nothing is deleted when settled or let go. **Letting go** (#437) is an amount, `forgiven_cents` (0 when nothing, with `forgiven_at` and `forgiven_note` of the latest let-go, set iff it is above 0): « Je ne me fais pas rembourser » lets go part or all of what is left (`POST /api/receivables/:id/forgive` `{ amountCents?, note? }`, everything left by default; letting go again adds to it) and the rest is still claimed and can still be repaid; « Réclamer à nouveau » (`DELETE …/forgive`) gives back all of it. The database refuses repaid + let go above the share (`receivables_settled_within_share`, a trigger on both `receivables` and `receivable_payments` that locks the item), a part let go outside 0…share and one without its day (checks); a share can't be lowered below repaid + let go. Items let go before #437 kept their meaning: let go = share − what was repaid then (migration `0052`). **A share repaid, even partly, or let go is never removed implicitly** (#438, the owner's choice of a refusal over a confirmation): removing it from « J'ai payé pour quelqu'un » (`replaceShares`), deleting it (`DELETE /api/receivables/:id`, either direction, a « Quelqu'un a payé pour moi » debt with its expense) or deleting the manual expense carrying it (`DELETE /api/transactions/manual/:id`) answers 409 « A share already repaid or let go cannot be removed » (`owed/settled.ts`), shown in French with the way out (« déliez d'abord ses remboursements, ou réclamez-la à nouveau »); otherwise its repayments would silently become income (or spending). The database says it too: `receivable_payments.receivable_id` is `ON DELETE RESTRICT` (migration `0050`), so any other path deleting a receivable with repayments fails. The two deliberate wholesale deletes remove the repayments first (`deletePaymentsOfShares`): undoing an import (its warning counts a shared row as edited, #371) and deleting an account (its shares go, the repayment transfers on other accounts stay, back in their category). **Repayments** (`receivable_payments`): a transfer (incoming for `owed_to_me`, outgoing for `i_owe`) linked to one or several items, partly or in full, never past the transfer nor past what is left; or « Réglé autrement » (no transaction, `paid_on`). Shares are integer cents: « moitié » / « parts égales entre n » (n counts the owner) are the total ÷ n half to even, the remainder on the owner (`owed/shares.ts`). The repayment suggestion (`owed/suggest.ts`): the oldest open items adding up exactly, else the closest without going over.
- **Offsets** (« Compenser », #484, revisiting #361's « No Compenser »): when a contact owes the owner and he owes them too, the two can be settled against each other without a transfer. The two directions stay recorded apart (each expense keeps its trace); an offset (`receivable_offsets`: contact, `amount_cents`, `offset_on`, `automatic`) settles `amount_cents` on each side as repayments without a transaction (`receivable_payments.offset_id`, `ON DELETE CASCADE`, check `receivable_payments_offset_without_transaction`), the oldest open items first (`owed/offset.ts`, `planOffset`: the smaller of the two totals) or the items the owner ticked. The deferred constraint triggers `receivable_offsets_balanced` / `receivable_payments_offset_balanced_*` (checked at commit, since the repayments are written after the offset) refuse an offset whose sides are not both the same amount, or that touches another contact's item; never past what is left is the existing `receivables_settled_within_share`. It is undone as one: « Annuler la compensation » deletes the offset and both sides go with it (`DELETE /api/receivables/offsets/:id`); unlinking one side alone is refused (409, and by the trigger). **Proposed automatically**: `GET /api/owed` lists the `offers` (people owed both ways, with the amount and the items), `GET /api/contacts/:id/offset` one person's; « Dépenses partagées », the person's page and « À revoir » show « Compenser avec Camille ? » (« Compenser 25 € » in one tap, then « Annuler » in the toast; « Ignorer » leaves it until the figures change, nothing is stored), and « Quelqu'un a payé pour moi » proposes it in its toast when the new debt makes the contact owe both ways. **« Toujours compenser »** (`contacts.always_offset`, the person's page, `PATCH /api/contacts/:id` `alwaysOffset`): a new share (`createShares`, `replaceShares`) or debt (`createDebt`) offsets at once, without asking (`automatic`), and turning it on offsets what is open now; a shared recurring item's charges don't trigger it (the offer shows instead). **Net view**: each list row has `netCents` (what they owe minus what he owes, both ways) shown as « net : vous doit 15 € » when the person is owed both ways, and the person's page says « Net : … » under the two tiles, with a « Compensations » card (the switch, each offset with « Annuler »). A later transfer settles only what is left: the repayment suggestion reads `leftCents`, which counts offsets as repaid. The deliberate wholesale deletes (`deletePaymentsOfShares`) delete every offset touching the shares they remove, whole, so the other side is owed again. Counting: an offset has no line in `transaction_lines` (like « Réglé autrement »), so no statistic moves. `POST /api/contacts/:id/offset` and the undo are session only; the MCP `owed_to_me` shows the net per person, the offers, each person's offsets and « dont 25 € compensés » on an item, and has no offset write tool (the owner offsets in the app).
- **The counting rule** lives in the `transaction_lines` view only (ADR 0005 R8; `amount_cents` is the owner's part, `gross_amount_cents` the line's own): an expense line counts minus its `owed_to_me` shares less their part let go (#437: the part let go is the owner's spending again, on the expense's day), a « Quelqu'un a payé pour moi » line minus the part the contact let go, and the part of a transfer linked as a repayment is dropped from its line (neither income nor spending; the rest stays in its category). So the owner's spending is his part on the day of the expense, repayments never move a month and never inflate income; unshared lines count exactly as before. Net worth is unchanged (cash only); what is owed either way is shown beside it, never in it. Reconciliation moves the shares and repayments of a manual transaction to its bank row, and undoing the import gives them back to the restored entry (#371); a bank row shared or linked by hand counts as edited in the undo's warning.
- **Shared recurring items** (the partner case): `recurring_items.share_contact_id` + `share_bps` (5000 = half) or `share_amount_cents`, `share_from` (default the current month's 1st), `PUT|DELETE /api/recurring/:id/share` (session only), a user column never touched by detection. `applyRecurringShares` (`owed/recurring-shares.ts`, after every detection, so every import, and when a share is set) gives each charge of the item booked from `share_from` (before `ended_on`, not one-off, not split, without a share of its own) its receivable (`origin = recurring`); `share_applied_at` makes a run look only at charges created since the last one, so a share removed by hand is never made again, and a new share changes later charges only. `RecurringItemDto.share`, `ownMonthlyEquivalentCents` / `ownYearlyCostCents` (the owner's part, « ta part 50 % »), and the recurring summary totals the owner's part. The month's expected repayments (`owed/expected.ts`): each booked charge's receivable, else the share of the charge still expected, on the contact's `repayment_day` or the month end.
- **API** (`owed/owed.controller.ts`): `GET /api/owed` (« Dépenses partagées »: `owedToMe` and `iOwe`, two lists per person with the oldest open item's age and the net both ways (`netCents`), the settled apart, the month's `expected` and the `offers`, #484), `GET|POST|PATCH|DELETE /api/contacts[/:id]`, `GET /api/contacts/:id/owed` (with its `offsets` and `offer`), `GET|POST /api/contacts/:id/offset` (#484), `DELETE /api/receivables/offsets/:id`, `POST /api/receivables` (shares), `POST /api/receivables/debts` (« Quelqu'un a payé pour moi »), `POST /api/receivables/payments` (link), `GET /api/receivables/repayment-options?transactionId=` (« C'est un remboursement › » / « Je rembourse … ›»: people, open items, suggestion), `GET /api/receivables/suggestions` (review queue « Remboursement de Camille ? »: recent unlinked transfers whose label matches a contact's words and whose amount is exactly some open items), `PATCH|DELETE /api/receivables/:id`, `POST|DELETE /api/receivables/:id/forgive`, `DELETE /api/receivables/payments/:id`, `PUT /api/transactions/:id/shares` (« J'ai payé pour quelqu'un »: replaces everyone's share; a share repaid or let go can't be removed, nor deleted, 409). Only the three `POST`s are `@TokenAccess('write')`; letting a debt go, unlinking, deleting, shares edited in place and contact settings are session-only (Claude never lets a debt go). `TransactionDto` carries `shares`, `repayments` and `paidByContactId` / `paidByContactName` (`accountId` / `accountName` null for « Quelqu'un a payé pour moi »).

A person's page (`/owed/$contactId`, « A. Une feuille par dépense »): both figures as tiles with the totals since the start, the expected repayments, « Il vous doit » / « Vous lui devez » (open items oldest first, then the history), each item opening `ReceivableSheet` (« Lier un remboursement » from a list of recent transfers with room left, « Réglé autrement » with an amount and a day, « Je ne me fais pas rembourser » / « Sam ne veut pas être remboursé » asking how much (#437: an `AmountField` filled with everything left, « Tout » puts it back, an optional note, « Abandonner 20 € »; what comes back into the spending and what is still owed said below it), « Réclamer à nouveau » while some is let go, the bar showing repaid and let go, the repayments with « Délier » (« Compensé » with « Annuler la compensation » for an offset's side) and « 20 € abandonnés » beside them). Rows carry a « Partagée » / « Remboursement » flag; « À revoir » starts with `RepaymentSuggestions` (« Remboursement de Camille ? », « Lier » in one tap, then the offers « Compenser avec Camille ? », #484); the sidebar's badge says what is owed to the owner (the dashboard's row left with #405); Abonnements shows « Partagé avec Camille · ta part 50 % · … / mois » and its totals read `ownYearlyCostCents`, the editor of a confirmed debit has « Partager avec quelqu'un » (`RecurringShareSection`); Réglages › Contacts (`/settings/contacts`) edits names, bank words, the usual day, archive and delete.
