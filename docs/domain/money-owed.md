# Money owed (Dépenses partagées, #359)

Feature details moved out of [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md) (#422). The domain rules, workflow conventions and architecture there apply here too.

- **Contacts** (`contacts`, `owed/contacts.schema.ts`: name unique case ignored, `match_labels` (words of their bank labels), `repayment_day`, `archived_at`): anyone the owner shares expenses with; no account, no sign-in. Created on the fly from a name (`contactName` wherever a contact is taken), managed in Réglages › Contacts; a contact with history is archived, never deleted (409). Names, labels and amounts are never logged.
- **Receivables** (`receivables`, `owed/owed.schema.ts`), one per expense and person, `direction` `owed_to_me` (the owner advanced the money: a share of an outgoing expense of one of his accounts, at most the expense with the other shares) or `i_owe` (« Quelqu'un a payé pour moi »: a contact paid; the owner's part is a manual expense **without an account**, `transactions.paid_by_contact_id`, check `(account_id is null) = (paid_by_contact_id is not null)`, always a manual expense, with its `i_owe` receivable of that part and the bill in `bill_cents`). A split expense's share names its part (`split_id`, required then); splitting, unsplitting or recategorizing the whole of a transaction that money owed hangs on is refused (400), as is changing the kind of a shared expense or an expense paid by a contact. State is computed, never stored: `open` (« à rembourser »), `repaid` (« remboursé ») or `forgiven` (« abandonné », `forgiven_at`, reversible « Réclamer à nouveau »); nothing is deleted when settled or let go. **A share repaid, even partly, or let go is never removed implicitly** (#438, the owner's choice of a refusal over a confirmation): removing it from « J'ai payé pour quelqu'un » (`replaceShares`), deleting it (`DELETE /api/receivables/:id`, either direction, a « Quelqu'un a payé pour moi » debt with its expense) or deleting the manual expense carrying it (`DELETE /api/transactions/manual/:id`) answers 409 « A share already repaid or let go cannot be removed » (`owed/settled.ts`), shown in French with the way out (« déliez d'abord ses remboursements, ou réclamez-la à nouveau »); otherwise its repayments would silently become income (or spending). The database says it too: `receivable_payments.receivable_id` is `ON DELETE RESTRICT` (migration `0047`), so any other path deleting a receivable with repayments fails. The two deliberate wholesale deletes remove the repayments first (`deletePaymentsOfShares`): undoing an import (its warning counts a shared row as edited, #371) and deleting an account (its shares go, the repayment transfers on other accounts stay, back in their category). **Repayments** (`receivable_payments`): a transfer (incoming for `owed_to_me`, outgoing for `i_owe`) linked to one or several items, partly or in full, never past the transfer nor past what is left; or « Réglé autrement » (no transaction, `paid_on`). Shares are integer cents: « moitié » / « parts égales entre n » (n counts the owner) are the total ÷ n half to even, the remainder on the owner (`owed/shares.ts`). The repayment suggestion (`owed/suggest.ts`): the oldest open items adding up exactly, else the closest without going over.
- **The counting rule** lives in the `transaction_lines` view only (ADR 0005 R8; `amount_cents` is the owner's part, `gross_amount_cents` the line's own): an expense line counts minus its `owed_to_me` shares (once let go, minus only what was repaid), a « Quelqu'un a payé pour moi » line minus what the contact let go, and the part of a transfer linked as a repayment is dropped from its line (neither income nor spending; the rest stays in its category). So the owner's spending is his part on the day of the expense, repayments never move a month and never inflate income; unshared lines count exactly as before. Net worth is unchanged (cash only); what is owed either way is shown beside it, never in it. Reconciliation moves the shares and repayments of a manual transaction to its bank row, and undoing the import gives them back to the restored entry (#371); a bank row shared or linked by hand counts as edited in the undo's warning.
- **Shared recurring items** (the partner case): `recurring_items.share_contact_id` + `share_bps` (5000 = half) or `share_amount_cents`, `share_from` (default the current month's 1st), `PUT|DELETE /api/recurring/:id/share` (session only), a user column never touched by detection. `applyRecurringShares` (`owed/recurring-shares.ts`, after every detection, so every import, and when a share is set) gives each charge of the item booked from `share_from` (before `ended_on`, not one-off, not split, without a share of its own) its receivable (`origin = recurring`); `share_applied_at` makes a run look only at charges created since the last one, so a share removed by hand is never made again, and a new share changes later charges only. `RecurringItemDto.share`, `ownMonthlyEquivalentCents` / `ownYearlyCostCents` (the owner's part, « ta part 50 % »), and the recurring summary totals the owner's part. The month's expected repayments (`owed/expected.ts`): each booked charge's receivable, else the share of the charge still expected, on the contact's `repayment_day` or the month end.
- **API** (`owed/owed.controller.ts`): `GET /api/owed` (« Dépenses partagées »: `owedToMe` and `iOwe`, two lists never netted, per person with the oldest open item's age, the settled apart, and the month's `expected`), `GET|POST|PATCH|DELETE /api/contacts[/:id]`, `GET /api/contacts/:id/owed`, `POST /api/receivables` (shares), `POST /api/receivables/debts` (« Quelqu'un a payé pour moi »), `POST /api/receivables/payments` (link), `GET /api/receivables/repayment-options?transactionId=` (« C'est un remboursement › » / « Je rembourse … ›»: people, open items, suggestion), `GET /api/receivables/suggestions` (review queue « Remboursement de Camille ? »: recent unlinked transfers whose label matches a contact's words and whose amount is exactly some open items), `PATCH|DELETE /api/receivables/:id`, `POST|DELETE /api/receivables/:id/forgive`, `DELETE /api/receivables/payments/:id`, `PUT /api/transactions/:id/shares` (« J'ai payé pour quelqu'un »: replaces everyone's share; a share repaid or let go can't be removed, nor deleted, 409). Only the three `POST`s are `@TokenAccess('write')`; letting a debt go, unlinking, deleting, shares edited in place and contact settings are session-only (Claude never lets a debt go). `TransactionDto` carries `shares`, `repayments` and `paidByContactId` / `paidByContactName` (`accountId` / `accountName` null for « Quelqu'un a payé pour moi »).

- **Web** (`components/owed/`, owner's choices of #361, 20 Nov): « Dépenses partagées » (`/owed`, in the sidebar's « Patrimoine » group after Crypto with what is owed to the owner as a badge, hidden at 0) holds two lists never netted (« B. A person's page (`/owed/$contactId`, « A. Une feuille par dépense »): both figures as tiles with the totals since the start, the expected repayments, « Il vous doit » / « Vous lui devez » (open items oldest first, then the history), each item opening `ReceivableSheet` (« Lier un remboursement » from a list of recent transfers with room left, « Réglé autrement » with an amount and a day, « Je ne me fais pas rembourser » / « Sam ne veut pas être remboursé », « Réclamer à nouveau », the repayments with « Délier »). The transaction editor's « Options » hold « J'ai payé pour quelqu'un › » (`ShareScreen`, « A. Un mode pour tous »: `PeoplePicker`, « Moitié » with one person / « Parts égales » with several / « Montants », each removable, « Ma part » live and red past the expense; saved after a short pause, toast « Annuler » back to how it was), « On me rembourse › » for money in when someone owes the owner and « Je rembourse › » for money out when the owner owes someone (#398 names; `RepaymentScreen`, « A. Liste cochée »: who, their open items with the suggestion ticked and an `AmountField` each, what is linked, what stays in the category, owed before → after, « Lier » off past the transfer or an item), « Je dois à Sam » on a « Quelqu'un a payé pour moi » row, and « ma part » under the amount. « Quelqu'un a payé pour moi » (`PaidForMeSheet`, « B. L'addition puis ma part », in four steps since #394 with `SheetSteps` and « Étape n sur 4 »: **Qui a payé** (`PeoplePicker`, a tap moves on), **Catégorie** (`CategoryPicker` of the expense kind, a tap moves on, « Nouvelle catégorie « … » » under a search that names none, `onCreate`), **Addition** (the bill, focused, the day, the label), **Ma part** (the owner's « A. Rows carry a « Partagée » / « Remboursement » flag; « À revoir » starts with `RepaymentSuggestions` (« Remboursement de Camille ? », « Lier » in one tap); the sidebar's badge says what is owed to the owner (the dashboard's row left with #405); Abonnements shows « Partagé avec Camille · ta part 50 % · … / mois » and its totals read `ownYearlyCostCents`, the editor of a confirmed debit has « Partager avec quelqu'un » (`RecurringShareSection`); Réglages › Contacts (`/settings/contacts`) edits names, bank words, the usual day, archive and delete. Shares are computed client-side the API's way (`owed/shares.ts`, half to even). **Choosing people** is one component everywhere (`PeoplePicker`, a list since #394, the owner: like the categories, never chips): « Avec qui ? » / « Qui a payé ? », a `SearchField` (« Rechercher ou ajouter un nom », Enter takes the first or adds the name), then the contacts as `OptionRow`s in one bordered list (`ContactAvatar`, the name, a check once chosen; archived ones left out), « Ajouter « Léa » » for a new name (« Nouveau contact » once chosen); `single` (« Quelqu'un a payé pour moi », a shared recurring item) is a radio list, otherwise a tap adds a person and a tap on a chosen one takes them out (`onRemove`, « J'ai payé pour quelqu'un »).
