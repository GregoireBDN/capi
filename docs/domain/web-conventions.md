# Web conventions and mobile layout

Feature details moved out of [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md) (#422). The domain rules, workflow conventions and architecture there apply here too.

## Authenticated layout and app shell

**Authenticated layout**: every signed-in page lives under the pathless layout route `routes/_app.tsx` (files in `routes/_app/`). Its `beforeLoad` guard (`requireUser` in `apps/web/src/lib/auth-guard.ts`) redirects anonymous visitors to `/auth` during SSR, before any protected markup renders (after trying to renew an expired access token, see [SSR session renewal](../../auth-and-sessions.md#ssr-session-renewal)), and exposes `user` through the route context. The shell (`apps/web/src/components/app-shell/`, #386, the owner's direction « A », after Azure DevOps) is a header on every width above the `@capi/ui` sidebar (a sheet below `md`). The header (`AppHeader`, `h-12`, `<header>` landmark, opening with a skip link « Aller au contenu » to `#main-content`, the `<main>`): ☰ (phones only: it opens the navigation sheet; no dot, the owner's choice in #393), the « Capi » wordmark (to the dashboard; `CapiWordmark`, #464, see [PWA › Brand](pwa.md)), the page's way back (`PageBack`, #404: the breadcrumb « Comptes › Compte courant » after the logo from `md` up, « ‹ Comptes » in place of the logo on phones), then the page's actions behind one « ⋮ » named « Actions de la page » (`PageHeader`, every width, only on a page that has some, a light filled `secondary` button so its dot shows, #393; no permanent quick entry nor search box: the owner tried both and found them unclear) and last the avatar (`AccountMenu`, a 40px disc in a 44px button beside « ⋮ »); a month page's month is not in it but in the bar at the top of the page's column (`MonthBarSlot`, #401). The sidebar (`AppSidebar`; glass like the header, no edge line, a soft shadow on the page's side (#505); the current page in the light blue of the month's tab (`--month-band`, its icon in the primary colour, the owner's « A · Bleu clair », #505), hover on the glass fill, rows 0.125rem apart, groups close under their titles) holds the navigation in titled groups (`NAV_GROUPS` in `app-shell/nav-items.ts`, names of #409: « Au quotidien » Tableau de bord, Mouvements (current on Opérations too, its sub-page, #393, with the « À revoir » count), Budget, Bilan du mois, Abonnements; « Patrimoine » Comptes, Épargne, Crypto, Dépenses partagées; « Outils » Relevés (the import page, `/import`); their icons, the owner's choices of #409: Mouvements `Activity`, Bilan du mois `NotebookPen` (also the dashboard's « Clôturer … »), Dépenses partagées `UsersRound` (also its empty page), Relevés `FileUp` (as every « Importer un relevé »); in a transaction's options « J'ai payé pour quelqu'un » is `HandCoins` (and the « Partagée » flag), « On me rembourse » / « Je rembourse », « Lier un remboursement » and the dashboard's repayments to link `Handshake`, « Quelqu'un a payé pour moi » and « Je dois à … » `ReceiptText` (and the row's flag of a debt), « Exceptionnelle » `Zap`), then at its foot (after Azure DevOps, the owner's choice) « Réglages » and, from `md` up, the button that folds it (« « » beside Réglages, « » » under it once folded); from `md` up it is a fixed column under the header (`min(16rem, 34vw)`; on phones a sheet 85 % wide, opened by ☰ or by a swipe to the right anywhere on the page and closed by a swipe to the left on it (#446, `useSidebarSwipe` in `app-shell/sidebar-swipe.ts`: a touch within 45° of horizontal is a swipe (a slanting one too, the owner's choice; steeper, the page's scroll), and from then on the page no longer scrolls under it (its touchmoves cancelled); the sheet and its backdrop follow the finger back and forth until it is let go, then settle open past half way or with a flick, on the sheet's own curve, `--sidebar-sheet-duration` / `--sidebar-sheet-ease` in `@capi/ui` `globals.css`, which also slides it in whole on ☰; never from a field, a dialog, a chart or anything that scrolls sideways or has its own sideways gesture (`touch-action` without `pan-x`, `data-no-swipe`: the day strip), nor from the left 44px of a Safari tab, its back swipe; in the installed app the left 24px (`BACK_SWIPE_EDGE`) are kept from iOS's back swipe (their touchstart cancelled, a tap there clicked by hand) so a swipe never goes back a page; with reduced motion the sheet still follows the finger and settles at once; a link closes it, painted, before the page changes, `closeSheetThenNavigate`, #419)), wide enough for « Dépenses partagées » whole from 320px (#409), its owed amount shown only where it fits beside the name (`@container/nav-item`). Add a section by creating `routes/_app/<name>.tsx` and an entry in a group of `NAV_GROUPS`. Settings pages are not in it (#261): they live under the pathless `routes/_app/_settings.tsx` (files in `routes/_app/_settings/`). **Réglages** (#300, `/settings`, `components/settings/SettingsPage.tsx`) is one list of sections like iOS Settings (`SettingsGroup` / `SettingsLinkRow` in `components/settings/SettingsList.tsx`: a card per group, rows with a tinted icon, the name and its current state under it, ›): Compte (`/settings/account`: the name edited in place, saved on blur or Enter with a toast offering « Annuler », the email as text, « Membre depuis »), Sécurité (`/settings/security`: `ChangePasswordCard`, `PasskeysCard`, `SessionsCard`), Catégories and Règles (their pages, URLs unchanged), Claude et accès (`/settings/access`, #329, `components/access/`: « Ce que Claude peut faire », « Connecter Claude » (« Claude sur ce Mac », « Claude sur le téléphone et claude.ai » while remote access is on, « Le Skill Capi » (#497, `SkillSheet`: downloads `/capi-skill.zip`, built from `skills/capi/` by `apps/web/scripts/build-skill-zip.mjs` before `vite dev` / `vite build`, git-ignored, served as a public static file since it holds methods only, with how to install it in claude.ai and Claude Code), « Script ou autre outil »), a stepped `ConnectSheet` (« Lire seulement » / « Lire et classer », the duration, then the token once inside a ready-to-paste `claude_desktop_config.json` and `claude mcp add` (`mcp-setup.ts`: the page's origin as `CAPI_API_URL`, the server path remembered in `localStorage`), never stored), then one list « Accès actifs » of tokens and grants whose detail (`AccessDetailSheet`) renames a token (`PATCH /api/access-tokens/:id`), revokes behind a confirmation and tells its calls in plain words (`access-labels.ts`, one sentence per route open to tokens; `GET /api/access-tokens/audit?tokenId=`)), Zone dangereuse (`/settings/danger`: `DeleteAccountCard`, last, alone). Every settings page but the list goes back to it, « Réglages › Sécurité » in the header (« ‹ Réglages » on phones: the layout's `DefaultPageBack`, given to each screen's `PageHeader`, #404); `/profile` redirects to `/settings`. **Réglages has one place** (the owner, #386: never repeated in a menu ⋮ or the account menu): the sidebar's foot (current on every settings page, `isSettingsPage`; on a phone ☰ then « Réglages »). The account menu (`AccountMenu`, the header's avatar, the owner's initials on the primary colour, `initials.ts`) is a popover on every width with who is signed in (name, email), « Apparence » (`ThemeChoice`, three segments, applied at once; not repeated on the Réglages page) and « Se déconnecter » (one click on a computer, « Se déconnecter ? » first on a phone; `useSignOut` in `lib/use-sign-out.ts`: `logout()`, then `/auth`; not repeated on the Réglages page): account, theme and sign-out two taps from any page. Every page uses the same full-width layout (`@container space-y-6` under `PageHeader`), never a narrower centred column. `/` only redirects (dashboard or login); there is no public landing page.

## Mobile layout conventions

- **Month pages** (#401, the owner's « B. Barre du mois »): the month is never a card nor a box on the page: it is a bar at the top of the page's column (‹ · 📅 month · ›, round grey arrows), out of the scrolling area under the header on every width (#418), moved by its arrows, never by a swipe (#446; `MonthNavigator`, `PeriodBar`, `MonthBarSlot`; see [Month comparisons](../../months-and-dashboard.md)).
- **Page header** (#246): every page opens with `PageHeader` (`app-shell/PageHeader.tsx`). Its title stays on one line on phones (`text-xl`, `md:text-2xl`; « Prévision fin de mois » is the longest that fits at 320px, so keep titles that short) and its description goes below it, smaller. Page actions are `actions` (`PageAction`: label, icon, `onSelect`, `primary`, `count`: what waits behind it, its number beside the item and a dot on the « ⋮ », hidden at 0, #393; what waits behind a control has one look and one place everywhere (the owner, #393), from `notification-badge.tsx` in `@capi/ui`: `NotificationCount`, a number astride a rounded control's top-right corner like an app's notification badge (centred on the 45° point of its arc, `radius` the control's), `NotificationDot`, the same place on an icon button as a dot (« ⋮ », the folded sidebar; from the button's size and radius, `--radius-lg` for `Button`, `--radius-md` for the sidebar), both ringed in the colour around them, and `CountPill` / `COUNT_PILL`, the same 18px number in a row (the sidebar entry, a menu item), white on the primary colour even under a hovered menu item): the items of the header's « Actions de la page » menu (« ⋮ », `AppHeader`, through a portal into its slot, `page-actions.tsx`), on every width, the main one first (the owner's choice on #386, after trying them beside the title, as icons and with a permanent « + Ajouter une dépense »: one header, the same everywhere); nothing beside the title. Each item has an icon of its own that says what it adds, never a bare « + » (the owner, #386: « Ajouter une dépense » a receipt, « Ajouter une opération » the Opérations arrows, « Ajouter un budget » its target, « Ajouter un revenu » a banknote, « Nouvelle enveloppe » an envelope with a plus, « Ajouter un contact » a person, « Ajouter une passkey » a fingerprint, « Ajouter une catégorie » a tag, « Nouvelle règle » a wand, crypto coins, a wallet key…). **Every « add » of a page is in that menu too** (the owner, #386): a section puts its own (« Nouvelle enveloppe », « Ajouter un avoir », « Ajouter une catégorie », « Ajouter une passkey »…) there with `useRegisterPageAction` (its sheet stays in the section; `null` takes it out when the section doesn't offer it), never as a button in a card or a bottom bar; an empty state keeps the button that fixes it, and a sheet or editor keeps its own (« Ajouter une part », « Ajouter un jeton »). Outside the shell (no header, e.g. a component test) they stay as buttons beside the title or in their section. A sub-page's way back is `back` (`PageBack`, #293, the one back link of the app since #324: account pages, a person's page of « Dépenses partagées » and the settings screens, which get « Réglages » from their layout, `DefaultPageBack`), a breadcrumb since #404 (the owner's « A », `Breadcrumb` in `@capi/ui`: `<nav aria-label="Fil d’Ariane">`, an ordered list, the separators `aria-hidden`): from `md` up « Comptes › Compte courant » in the header after the logo, the parent a 44px link, the page as text (`aria-current="page"`, its `PageHeader` title or `crumb`, at most 18rem, cut with …; an account page names its kind (`accountKindLabel`), « Comptes › Assurance vie », a crypto account its source, « Plateforme crypto », « Wallet crypto », « Compte crypto », the owner's own name staying the title, `compactTitle`: `text-xl` on every width, two lines at most, the owner's choice on #408); on phones (`backOnPhones`) only « ‹ Comptes », in place of the logo (☰ stays), the page's title right below saying where you are; the title is unchanged; outside the shell (and before hydration) a visually hidden link above the title; never a link placed by hand above the header. The account page returns where it was opened from: `?from=` (`accounts`, `savings`, `crypto`, `net-worth`, `dashboard`, `month-review`, `review`; `components/accounts/account-origin.ts`) names the page and `?month=` keeps the month it showed (« ‹ Épargne » → `/savings?month=…`), « ‹ Comptes » without one; every link to `ROUTES.account` passes `search={accountLinkSearch(origin)}`. Never put a button next to the title by hand. Actions tied to one section (a card, a sticky bottom bar) stay in it; a month page's « Filtres » (Opérations': « À revoir », #406) sit at the end of the title's row (`PageTitleEnd`, #401), never placed there by hand. The dashboard's only page action is « Ajouter une dépense »: its « À faire » leads to the import when the data is stale.
- **Read now and then, one tap away** (#447, the owner's « C. Lignes comme Réglages »): on an account page, a panel read now and then (linked transfers, imports, debits, statements, movements, keys, the typed values) is not a card of the page but a row of its « Plus d’informations » card (`AccountDetails` in `components/accounts/AccountDetails.tsx`: icon, name, what it holds in a few words, in amber when something waits in it, ›), opening its own screen (full screen on phones, a centred window elsewhere). A panel read every time, or with something to do now (« À préciser »), stays on the page; a moved panel's main action stays in the page's « ⋮ » (« Importer un export »). Details: [accounts](accounts.md).
- **Amounts** (#326): every euro amount is typed in `AmountField` (`components/amount/`): unsigned exact text read into cents by the caller (`parseEurosToCents`, `signedCents`), the decimal pad (`inputMode="decimal"`), anything that isn't an amount refused by keeping the value. The sign is never typed: a field that takes negatives has a « − » / « + » button before the amount (a « - » typed or pasted on a keyboard sets it), `useSignedText` for a field kept as signed text; rule conditions are magnitudes with « Sortie / Entrée » (`rules/rule-form.ts` `boundsOf`, a lone magnitude stopping at 0, a saved condition the fields can't show kept as it is). Crypto quantities and prices are not amounts.
- **Dates** (#488): every date field is `DateField` (`@capi/ui`), never a native `<input type="date">` (its picker followed the browser's language and look, English in Chrome on a Mac, the wheel on iOS): the day as `jj/mm/aaaa` in a field like `Input` that opens the app's `Calendar` in French (`fr`, weeks from Monday, the month and the year in lists) in a popover, or a bottom sheet on phones (no keyboard, so it climbs nothing); days of 44px at least on touch screens. **Every day is a round pastille, never an oval** (the owner, #488: the whole cell is the button, the pastille inside it is square by its width, at most `--cell-size`), drawn by `CalendarDayButton` for every `Calendar` (`DateField` and `DayStrip` alike), each state told apart at a glance: a day to pick on a soft filled pastille, a day of the month before or after on a fainter one, today ringed in the primary colour, the chosen day filled with it, a day that can't be picked (outside `min` / `max`, e.g. the future with `max={today}`) with no pastille, faded and struck through. `--day-text` sizes the figures. Its value is a calendar day `YYYY-MM-DD` (`""` when none), built from the picked date's local parts (`lib/calendar-day.ts`), never a `Date` moved by a time zone. « Aujourd’hui » picks today in Europe/Paris (hidden when out of bounds), `clearable` offers « Effacer » on an optional field (an objective's « Échéance »), `title` names the bottom sheet (the field's label); a `FieldLabel htmlFor` names it, `aria-invalid` and `FieldError` work as on `Input`. In tests, `pickDay(user, field, 'YYYY-MM-DD')` (`apps/web/src/test/date-field.ts`) picks a day as the owner would and the field's text is `dd/mm/yyyy`. The day chips of a manual entry (`DayStrip`) stay as they are.
- **Single choices** (#289): one choice among a few (a comparison, a period, a kind, a filter of the import preview) is a `SegmentedControl` (`@capi/ui`: glass track, selected segment raised, 44px, never empty, arrow keys select), `fullWidth` when it is alone on its row; never an outline `ToggleGroup`. Choices that can be empty or multiple stay chips.
- **Choosing a category** (#396, the owner's « A. Tuiles », without the tiles' second line, then pastel tiles without border like a category's badge): one picker everywhere a category is chosen, `CategoryPicker` (`components/categories/CategoryPicker.tsx`): fixed at the top the kinds as `ChoiceTiles` (`@capi/ui`: an icon over « Dépense » / « Revenu » / « Virement », the chosen one tinted with its colour, its text in it: the expense a soft orange, the income green, the transfer blue; `KIND_TILES`) and a `SearchField`; under them the list that scrolls (`data-sheet-scroll`), `OptionGroup` / `OptionRow` rows: « Habituels » (the quick entry's usual labels with their category), « Les plus utilisées » (`preferred`, e.g. the envelopes, then the kind's most used from `GET /api/transactions/manual/suggestions`), « Toutes les catégories » A–Z with `CategoryIcon`, and an optional « Sans catégorie » row (`none`); under a search that names no category, « Nouvelle catégorie « … » » where the caller can create one (`onCreate`, #394: « Quelqu'un a payé pour moi »). A search looks in every kind offered, the shown one first, other kinds named under the row (picking one switches the kind); Enter takes the first result. A picker for one kind (`kinds`) has no tiles (#368: a rule, a budget, the target of a deletion, a recurring item, « Classer en dépense »). Archived categories never show but the one chosen (`pickableCategories`). A step of an editor shows it in place (the « Catégorie » step of the transaction editor, adding or editing, and the rule's « Classer dans »); a form shows a `CategoryField` (the chosen icon and name, ›) opening it in its own panel (`CategoryPickerSheet`, full screen on phones), a tap picking and closing it: the split parts, « Classer dans… » of the selection, « Quelqu'un a payé pour moi », the recurring and budget editors, the import preview's rows, the target of a category's deletion. No native select or chips for categories (the wide pages' `CategoryMenu` stays). Tests pick with `pickCategory` / `chooseCategory` (`src/test/category-picker.ts`).
- **One-hand use.** Primary actions sit in sticky bottom bars (`bottom-[calc(1rem+env(safe-area-inset-bottom))]`), kept to one sideways-scrolling row on short screens with the `short-screen:` variant (≤ 600px tall, `apps/web/src/globals.css`); on phones every panel that used to open from the bottom is a full-screen page (`side="screen"`, below). Toasts stay clickable over an open sheet and clicking one does not close it (`Toaster` / `SheetContent` in `@capi/ui`), so an « Annuler » for an edit made inside a sheet works (#249). Toasts are the owner's « B. Teinte douce » (#380, `packages/ui/src/components/sonner.tsx`): the app's card (popover, border, radius), the icon on a tile of its tone (success `chart-delta-good`, error `destructive` with a red border, warning, info, loading muted), the second line muted, the action (« Annuler », « Recharger ») an outline button 44px high on touch screens; never a toast filled with its colour. They rise above a sticky bottom bar instead of covering it: mark every such bar `data-sticky-bottom-bar` (`BulkActionsBar`, the month close, « Ajouter une catégorie »), and the toaster keeps `--toast-bottom-clearance` on the root (`toast-clearance.ts`). The header (`AppHeader`) is one `h-12` row on every width (`--app-header`), and a month page adds its bar under it (#401): **the header and the month bar never scroll** (#418): the shell is as tall as the window (`body:has([data-app-shell])` in `globals.css`, the body `overflow: clip`), and the page scrolls in its own area (`#app-scroll` in `routes/_app.tsx`, `relative`, `overflow-x-hidden`, its scrollbar starting under them; `app-shell/app-scroll.ts` for code that scrolls the page: `scrollAppTo`, `appScrollTop`, never `window.scrollTo`), so a page's sticky headers use `top-0`; the router resets that area on a new page and restores it on back / forward (`scrollToTopSelectors`, `data-scroll-restoration-id`); its content starts `pt-5` under the bars on every page and pads its end for the home indicator; from `md` up the sidebar's fixed column starts under the header. From `md` up the sidebar is always on screen; the button at its foot or Cmd/Ctrl+B folds it down to its icons (page names as tooltips, a short rule between the groups in place of their titles, the `NotificationDot` on Mouvements while there is something to review; icons 1.1rem in 44px buttons, the 3.5rem column centred under the header's logo) and back, remembered in the `sidebar_state` cookie read during SSR.
- **Safe areas and keyboard.** `Sheet` pads its edge-to-edge sides with `env(safe-area-inset-*)`, the fixed sidebar (≥ md, e.g. a phone in landscape) is offset from the notch, toasts are offset too. **On phones every panel is a full-screen page** (#385, the owner's « B. Plein écran » with « Ronds »: a bottom sheet climbed a second time once the iOS keyboard was up): `SheetContent side="screen"` (`editorSide(isMobile)`, below; stepped flows use `screen` on phones and a dialog elsewhere), opaque behind, the page under it locked (`position: fixed` body), opening without the keyboard (it focuses itself, never its first field, unless the caller focuses one: the quick entry's amount). Its top is `SheetBar` (`@capi/ui`: a round ✕, or ‹ for a step back, the title on two lines at most, a round main action `SheetBarAction`, ✓ by default or › for « Suivant », greyed out while it can't be used, then the description; a stepped editor puts its steps there instead, `SheetSteps`: one round icon per step with its name, a line filling up to the current one, earlier and reachable steps a tap away, later ones greyed out until the earlier ones are filled in, the owner's request in #385), its content scrolls in a body marked `data-sheet-scroll` (`FormSheetBody`, `SheetScroll` in `components/sheets/form-sheet.tsx`); the actions are never in a footer: the main one is the bar's round button (`EditorHeader`), the others (« Supprimer », « Retirer ») go at the end of the body (`FormSheetMoreActions`). A flow that asks one question per screen (`AddCryptoSheet`, `AddPrepaidSheet`) keeps the question as a heading at the top of the body. The keyboard covers the end of the page and moves nothing (`useKeyboardInset`, `sheet-keyboard.ts`): as a field is tapped, before the keyboard is up, a field in the lower 60 % of the screen is scrolled into the part the keyboard leaves free (`FREE_ABOVE_KEYBOARD_SHARE`, `revealFieldScrollTop`) with room at the body's end (`data-keyboard-expected`), so iOS never pans the page; while the keyboard is open the page fits the visible area (`--viewport-top` / `--viewport-height`, the end keeping room for the iOS 26 accessory bar, `--keyboard-accessory`: 84px in a Safari tab, 50px in the installed app) and only a field the keyboard hides is scrolled. Android resizes the page (`interactive-widget=resizes-content`). On wider screens the same panels are **centred modals, never a panel beside the page** (#394, the owner: a side panel felt like another way of working than the quick entry's dialog): `editorSide(isMobile)` → `screen` / `center` (`SheetContent side="center"`: in the middle of the page like `DialogContent`, as tall as its content up to the window's, `EDITOR_STEADY_HEIGHT` keeping one height for an editor whose screens differ, steps or a list to search: `CategoryPickerSheet`, « Quelqu'un a payé pour moi »; the transaction editor is a dialog of its own height), the stepped flows staying dialogs, all **built the same way** (the owner's request in #385: the same bar, steps and actions, no footer, a toast's « Annuler » clickable over them; the corner ✕ of `SheetContent` / `DialogContent` hides itself when a `SheetBar` is inside); the month bar's « Filtres » on a narrow window stays a bottom sheet following `--keyboard-inset` (#205).
- **Empty states** (#214): every empty page, card or chart area uses `EmptyState` (`@capi/ui`, `page` or `inline`): an icon, one short line, why, and the action that fixes it (import, add an account, set a target, « Voir septembre 2026 » from an empty month through `NearestMonthButton` / `nearestMonthWithData` in `components/month/`). No block of 0,00 € tiles or empty chart frame without a word: hide them. Abonnements runs the recurring detection by itself once when it has nothing to show (as do bank imports and, with `--commit`, the history import).
- **Audit.** `apps/web/e2e/mobile-layout.spec.ts` (run with `playwright.mobile.config.ts`, never in CI) visits every page at 320/375/390/412/414px and 844×390 in light and dark, and fails on horizontal scroll, hit areas < 44px, form fonts < 16px and text sticking out of its box. It needs a running stack on a throwaway database with data and the owner's credentials in `MOBILE_AUDIT_EMAIL`/`MOBILE_AUDIT_PASSWORD`; the API's global rate limit (60 requests/min per IP) throttles a full run, so run it a page or project at a time (`-g`, `--project`) or against a throwaway API instance whose limit is raised.

The look the owner chose for the whole app (#503, recipe « A · Givré », the month bar of #502 extended). Tokens and utilities in `packages/ui/src/globals.css` (#504), shown over a busy page in Storybook « Foundations / Verre ». **Fields** (#507, the owner's « B · Creusé »): every field of `@capi/ui` (`Input`, `SearchField`, `InputGroup`, `Textarea`, `PasswordInput`, `InputOTP`, `DateField`, `NativeSelect`, `FilterControl` / `FilterToggle`) is a `glass-field` hollow while empty and rises once filled (`glass-field-filled`; `:placeholder-shown`, so a field without placeholder gets « »); a field-like button hand-rolled in `apps/web` (e.g. `CategoryPicker`'s trigger) takes the same classes from `fieldSurface` (or `fieldGroupSurface` for a box around its control, `@capi/ui`) and `glass-field-focus` while its list is open.
