# Investments, positions and savings

Feature details moved out of [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md) (#422). The domain rules, workflow conventions and architecture there apply here too.

## Value snapshots

- **Investment accounts** (brokerage, life_insurance, employee_savings, crypto) are tracked by **dated value snapshots** (`balance_snapshots.source`: `typed` for statements and values typed by hand or through the MCP, `computed` for a crypto account's daily close, #366). A crypto account's value is always holdings × price, never typed: `PUT|POST bulk` on a crypto account and `PUT|DELETE` of a computed point answer 400 (`COMPUTED_VALUE_MESSAGE`), and the MCP `add_investment_snapshots` refuses crypto accounts.

## A PEA's lines

- **A PEA's lines** (#413, [ADR 0006](../adr/0006-security-positions.md), `apps/api/src/positions/`; the owner's « A. Comme le Crypto », 21 Nov): what a PEA holds is a series of dated **position statements** (`position_statements`: account, `as_of`, `cash_cents` « Liquidités », source `typed` / `mcp`; `position_lines`: security, quantity `numeric(38, 18)` > 0, PRU `unit_cost_eur` `numeric(38, 12)` as printed, nullable), a statement being the whole account that day (a line left out was sold); the account holds, on a day, its latest statement on or before it. Securities by **ISIN** (`securities`: `isin` checked by the database's `isin_is_valid` (format and Luhn check digit, migration `0046`) and by `positions/isin.ts` / `web lib/isin.ts` / `mcp src/isin.ts`; the country prefix is not checked, so fixtures and the demo use `ZZ…` ISINs, never a real one), name, optional ticker) and their **prices** (`security_prices`: one exact EUR price per security and day, `manual` > `statement` > `provider`, never replaced by a weaker source); no public price source (none is key-less and reliable): prices come with statements or are typed (`PUT /api/securities/:isin/prices/:day`). Positions only on `POSITION_ACCOUNT_TYPES` (`brokerage`; a trigger, `position_statements_account_type`). **The value follows the priced lines** (`positions/valuation.ts`, `revalue` in `positions.repository.ts`, run in the transaction of every write): one `computed` `balance_snapshot` per statement day and per price day of a held security, Σ round half to even of quantity × latest price on or before the day, plus the cash; none on a day a line has no price; **a typed value wins its day** (`computed` is never written over it; typing a value replaces a computed one; deleting a typed one gives the day back to the lines; a computed one is never deleted, `LINES_VALUE_MESSAGE`). A line's cost = quantity × PRU, its result = value − cost, both computed, never stored; the account's invested amount stays the #370 rule (it wins), the lines' cost being its fallback (`lines`). `GET /api/accounts/:id/positions` (the lines as of today with price, value, result, the cash, totals, statements newest first); `PUT /api/accounts/:id/positions/statements/:asOf` (a whole statement, its prices that day's `statement` prices, unknown securities created with their name; write token, the MCP's `sync_security_positions`) and `POST …/preview` (read: new / changed / removed / unchanged lines, cash and value before → after, `typedValueCents`, `unchanged`); session only: `DELETE …/statements/:asOf`, the hand edits `PUT|DELETE …/positions/lines/:isin` and `PUT …/positions/cash`, **made as of today** (today's statement, or a copy of the latest one dated today: the past is never rewritten), and manual prices. Logs count lines and values only. **PEA facts** (§ 7): `accounts.opened_on` (PEA, life insurance, employee savings; check `accounts_opened_on_type`; `PATCH /api/accounts/:id` `openedOn`, not in the future) gives the 5-year date (+ 60 months); its invested amount is shown against `PEA_CEILING_CENTS` (150 000 €). The web (`components/positions/`; the owner's review of 21 Nov: less text, two lines a row, more figures unfolded): the summary (`InvestmentSummary` « Valeur au 31 oct. », then `PeaFacts` at its end, two blocks alike: « Plafond … investis sur 150 000 € » with its bar and « Encore … possibles », and « 5 ans » with its date, a bar of the time gone and « Encore 4 mois » / « Atteints » (`timeLeftText`), the whole block opening the opening date, « Date d’ouverture › » while unset), then **« Lignes »** (`LinesCard`, like the Crypto page's « Actifs »: the split bar and its legend, one row per ETF largest first on two lines (ticker disc from 18rem, the name and the value, from 1 000 € without cents (`rowAmount`); the parts and the result rate; ⌄), unfolded: the ISIN, then six tiles, two a row on a phone, three from 560px (Investi, Résultat, Cours with its day (« · manuel »), PRU, Part, Quantité), « Modifier la ligne », « Nouveau cours »; « Liquidités » on two lines last (a tap edits it); « Total » (the lines' value, « acheté 12 653 € », the difference); then « Historique (n) » folded, the statements, each deletable), the linked transfers, « Évolution », and « Valeurs » (computed ones « D’après vos lignes », edited by typing a value of the day, never deleted). « Ajouter une ligne » is a page action (first once the PEA has lines, after « Mettre à jour la valeur » before). `LineEditor` (ISIN with its check, name, quantity, PRU; « Retirer la ligne »), `PriceSheet`, `CashSheet`, all full screen on phones. The demo PEA has its last 6 month ends as statements of three `ZZ…` ETFs.

## Invested amount

- **One invested amount per account** (#370, ADR 0005 R2; `investments/performance.ts` `investedAt` / `investedOn`, used by `InvestmentsService` for the account page, Épargne, the lists and the MCP, the Crypto page reading the same replay): a non-crypto investment account's invested amount on a day is its **latest typed point** on or before it (the invested base, or the contributions typed on a value, whichever is later; the base on the same day) **plus the bank transfers linked to it after that point** (#296, `transactions.counterpart_account_id`), up to that day; without a typed point, the transfers linked so far; null with neither, except a PEA whose lines all have a PRU, which then takes their cost plus its cash (`lines`, ADR 0006 § 6) (`contributionsSource` `base` / `typed` / `links` / `lines`, `contributionsDate` the typed point's day, `linkedTransfersCount`, `unknownCostCount` the linked transfers of unknown amount; `linkedAfterValueCents` for transfers after the latest value, out of the gain). Without links the typed value stays (employee savings paid by the employer). A crypto account's is the replay of its coins (#373, [crypto](../../crypto.md#cost-basis-and-observed-movements), `contributionsSource: 'coins'`). `balance_snapshots.contributions_cents` holds **typed figures only**: no valuation and no close writes it (the close writes null; migration `0044` nulled the computed ones). The account page lists the linked transfers (`LinkedTransfers`) and charts value vs these contributions (`contributionsHistory`); Épargne gives the stock once, in « Livrets et placements » (#407: the total and the investments' result), and the linked transfers per account stay on the account pages (`LinkedTransfers`, `MovementsCard`).
- **Invested base** (#358, #360, narrowed by #373; `investments/performance.ts` `takesInvestedBase`): a PEA, a life insurance or employee savings (never a crypto account nor a cash account: check constraint `accounts_invested_base_type`, 400) takes what the owner invested up to a day, `accounts.invested_base_cents` / `invested_base_on` (« Investi au … »), a typed point of `investedAt` (above): the transfers linked after its day add on top, a value typed later with its own invested amount wins; the values stay untouched; with a base dated after the latest value, the base as of today. `PUT /api/accounts/:id/invested-base` (`@TokenAccess('write')`), `POST …/invested-base/preview` (`read`, now → with it: invested, result, rate), `DELETE …/invested-base` (session only); `AccountDto` carries `investedBaseCents` / `investedBaseOn`, the performance `investedBase`. The web types it with `InvestedSheet` from the account's summary (below). The MCP: `set_invested_amount` (refuses crypto accounts in French). Migration `0044` moved a crypto account's base (#358) to the cost of its opening when it held one asset (the base less the coins linked to it up to its day) and dropped the others, printing their counts only.

## The summary of every investment account

- **The summary of every investment account** (#360, the owner's direction « A » with « Barre »; `components/accounts/InvestmentSummary.tsx`, the same on a PEA, a life insurance, employee savings, an exchange, a manual crypto account and a wallet, where it replaced `PerformanceTiles`, `HoldingsPanel`'s tiles and the wallet's own summary): the value once (« Valeur », « Valeur du wallet » with its live holdings), for a value typed by hand its day in the title (« Valeur au 31 oct. », `asOf`; the owner, #413: no caption under it, an old value having its warning), its change since the previous value as short as it can say it (`changePeriodText`: « −78,13 € en un mois » between two month ends, « depuis hier », else « +415,76 € depuis le 30 sept. »), a wallet's status lines; then « Investi 13 800 € » on a band with the value as one bar, what was put in (grey) and the result (green) or what was lost (red); « Résultat » (euros and rate) and « Sur 1 an » as two tiles abreast (one alone at full width); where the invested amount comes from (`investedSourceText` in `accounts/invested.ts`: « D’après vos exports, au prix d’achat », « Au prix d’achat des cryptos reçues de vos plateformes », « Saisi au 2 janv. 2026 + 3 virements liés », « D’après 14 virements liés », « Saisi sur la valeur du 31 oct. 2026 », « · hors 0,1 BTC sans coût connu »): where it is typed (`takesInvestedBase`: a PEA, a life insurance, employee savings) a pencil in the « Investi » block opens `InvestedSheet`, which says it (« Aujourd’hui : saisi au … »), the summary saying nothing (#413, the owner: too much text); elsewhere (crypto) one line under the tiles; then the note on transfers after the latest value. A crypto account's bar and result count only its coins of known cost. One word for the money put in, « Investi », and one for value − invested, « Résultat » (the owner's choice: « Plus-value » reads wrong when negative), in the tiles, the value editor, the values list, the chart, the lists, the Crypto page, Épargne and the MCP output; never « Versements » nor « plus-value ». An unknown invested amount is never a « — »: one line (« Montant investi pas encore renseigné : le gain ou la perte s’affichera dès qu’il le sera. » and « Renseigner le montant investi »; a crypto account: that the cost of its coins is not known yet, to answer in « À préciser »). `InvestedSheet` (a full-screen page on phones, #385) takes it in two steps: « Montant investi » (`AmountField`, « Suivant » greyed out while empty, « Retirer » when there is a base), then « Date » (`SegmentedControl`: the latest value's day as « 31 oct. », « Aujourd’hui », « Autre » with a date field), saved as the invested base with « Annuler » in its toast. A value's own invested amount stays under « Plus d’options » in `SnapshotEditor` (« Investi à cette date », for the statements that print it). Every investment page reads in the same order: summary, what it holds (`HoldingsPanel` and the source card, or `LinkedTransfers`, or « Mouvements »), the evolution chart (value and invested), the values (each with the invested amount that counts, `contributionsHistory`); a crypto account has no values list and no value editor or « Coller un historique » (#366): its « Évolution » is `CryptoHistory`, read only, ending on today's live value, and its summary's change is since yesterday's close.

## Épargne

- **Épargne** (#407, the owner's « A. Jauge », then « far less text »: every sentence became a figure with a two-word label, or went; no chart of the months, the month bar moves between them). One word per idea, on the page and in the MCP: **« Épargné »** (income − expenses, transfers between own accounts excluded) with **« Cible »** (the monthly target) and the share of the income; **« Mis de côté »** (what left the checking accounts for savings accounts and investments: `movedToSavingsCents` + `movedToInvestmentsCents`, the page month's in `SavingsOverviewDto.moved`); **« Livrets et placements »** (what is held). Top to bottom (`components/savings/`), one card per row at every width (the owner: two columns of cards read worse on a computer). From `WIDE_LAYOUT_REM` (36rem) of page (`useWideLayout`, a `ResizeObserver` on the page: a 1280 px window with the sidebar open) the cards take the owner's computer layout « A. Rangée de chiffres » (chosen from three prototypes on PR #415, after « the figures are too big and too few on a computer »): the month as one row of five figures at a reading size (Épargné with « +254 € vs cible » under it, Taux d’épargne, Mis de côté, Compte courant, Moyenne with the dots; while the month runs: the same days' gap, last month, no checking account) over a thin gauge, the month's « Mis de côté » card left out; every account as one table row (badge and name, type, value in whole euros, result in %, opening its page), the total and the result in the header; every goal as one table row (name, bar, saved / target, « 350 €/mois », the month reached), « 782 €/mois · marge +162 € » in the header; the 12 months of « Mis de côté » as one strip. Below that width, the phone's cards: `MonthCard` (« Épargné » or « Épargné à ce jour », its status chip `monthStatus`: Cible atteinte, Sous la cible, En cours, « Jusqu’au 20 oct. » for a past month whose data stops early, none without a target; the figure; a gauge with « Cible 860 € » (else « Moyenne ») as a mark; two `Stat`s: « +254 € vs cible » and « 27 % des revenus », or while the month runs « +511 € vs 1–11 oct. » and last month « 854 € ✓ Octobre », never a verdict nor a rate on a partial month; « Hors exceptionnel 698 € »; the last 6 full months as dots « 5/6 » and « Moyenne 944 € » (`projectionAverageCents`, the one average); the target edited in place (`TargetForm`) from « ⋮ » « Modifier la cible » or « Fixer une cible »; an empty month says so with `NearestMonthButton`), `MonthMovedCard` (« Mis de côté » with the month as a chip, the figure, « Épargné » and the split Livrets / Placements as bars, then « Compte courant −46 € », saved − put aside, once the month is complete); then « Ce que vous avez »: `StockCard` (the total, « +3 910 € de résultat », Livrets / Placements as one bar, the 3 largest accounts with `AccountList` then « Voir les n comptes » to Comptes), `GoalsSection` (« 782 € par mois » the active goals need together (`goalsMonthlyNeed`) and « +162 € de marge » left by the average; per goal its name and « 350 €/mois » (`requiredMonthlyCents`) or « ✓ Atteint », the bar, « 1 200 € / 4 000 € » and the month it is reached at this pace with a calendar icon, green before its deadline, red after (`goalWhen`), or « Hors de portée » / « Échéance passée »; the whole goal opens `GoalEditor`, « Voir les archivés » last; each goal is projected at the whole average, as the API does) and `YearMovedCard` (« Mis de côté » over the overview's 12 months, same lines). Small pieces in `savings/figures.tsx` (`Stat`, `FigureRow`, `SplitBar`, `StatusChip`). No card description, and the `PeriodNote` only when the month is not a full one.
