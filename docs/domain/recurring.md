# Recurring items (Abonnements)

Feature details moved out of [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md) (#422). The domain rules, workflow conventions and architecture there apply here too.

- `recurring_items` are series found by the detector (`apps/api/src/recurring/detect.ts`, one per account + merchant key + direction; transfers and one-offs never count): `suggested`, `confirmed` (a real recurring item) or `dismissed` (not one, never suggested again). A detection run refreshes the stats only, never the user's columns (`name`, category, fixed-cost flag, cadence override, `notes`, `status`, `ended_on`, `term_months`, `renewal_kept_on`). A series without a charge for `STALE_PERIODS` (1.5) periods is inactive. Abonnements opens with `RecurringKpis` (the owner's « A. Un total, une barre », #314): the monthly total of the confirmed debits still counted, its year and number of charges, then one stacked bar and a legend of the page's own split, Abonnements · Autres charges fixes · Charges variables (`recurringParts`: disjoint, each part's monthly amount its section header's, so they add up to the total; empty parts left out, the card hidden without any); recurring incomes stay in their section, not in the card.
- **Prepaid subscriptions** (#314, `recurring/prepaid.ts`): a plan paid once, upfront, for a term (`recurring_items.term_months`, 2–60, the app offers 12 / 24 / 36). Declared by the owner from an expense (`POST /api/recurring/prepaid`: the item of its account + merchant becomes, or is created as, `confirmed`, no second occurrence needed; « Prépayé › » in the transaction editor's « Options », a list of terms each with its monthly cost and renewal, a tap saving it (`PrepaidTermControl`, #398), « Ajouter un abonnement prépayé » on Abonnements, `AddPrepaidSheet` in three steps; the term changed or cleared with `PATCH /api/recurring/:id` `termMonths`; `DELETE /api/recurring/:id` removes a prepaid item, the undo of a declaration that created it; all session only). Its payments are the transactions linked to it (`transactions.prepaid_item_id`, `ON DELETE SET NULL`, cleared when the kind leaves `expense`); detection runs link a later charge of the merchant within `PREPAID_RENEWAL_WINDOW_DAYS` (30) of the renewal, the closest, as its next payment. Its price is the latest payment, monthly cost = payment ÷ term and yearly = payment × 12 ÷ term (integer cents, half to even), renewal (`renewalDate`, also `nextExpectedDate`) = latest payment + term (`addMonths`, clamped to the month length); inactive once the renewal window is over without a charge. The forecast, the expected costs and the upcoming debits expect the renewal, then one every term, never on or after `ended_on` (« Résilier » defaults to the renewal date). The payment counts in its month's spending and budget, but `transaction_lines.is_one_off` is true for it, so averages and budget suggestions leave it out like a one-off; budget lines list it (`prepaid`, « dont 180,00 € prépayés pour 24 mois »). `renewalReminder` is on from `PREPAID_REMINDER_DAYS` (30) before the renewal to the day, unless cancelled or kept (`keepRenewal` stores `renewal_kept_on`): `RenewalReminders` on Abonnements with « Garder » / « Résilier ». Rows show « Prépayé 24 mois », the monthly cost and « payé 180,00 € pour 24 mois »; transaction rows a flag. The MCP `subscriptions` tool shows the term, the monthly equivalent and the renewal date.
- **Cancellation** (« Résilier », #270) is an end date, not a status: `recurring_items.ended_on` is the **first day no charge is expected any more** (past, today or future), on `confirmed` items only (`PATCH /api/recurring/:id` with `endedOn`, `null` reactivates; 400 on other statuses; refused to access tokens: 403, so the MCP never cancels). The DTO carries `endedOn`, `ended` (`endedOn` ≤ today in Europe/Paris) and `chargedAfterEnd` (latest occurrence booked on or after `endedOn`, a cancellation that did not go through; the end date is never cleared automatically). From `ended_on` an item is out of the forecast and the expected costs (`expectedOccurrences` expects nothing on or after it), and once `ended` out of the yearly / fixed-cost / « Abonnements » totals (`summary`, `endedCount`) and the upcoming debits; before it, it counts as usual. The web shows « Résilié · fin le … » in its section until then, then « Abonnements résiliés » (collapsed, opened when one was charged after its end), with « Réactiver » and « Modifier la date » in the editor; « Résilier » (`EndRecurringSheet`: « Aujourd'hui », « Après la prochaine échéance », any date, note) is for debits only, and every change's toast offers « Annuler ».
